# Retailer sales deck — spec

**Audience:** chain exec (CEO/CMO/commercial director). Language: CZ.
**Story:** retailer-as-monetizer — "your new revenue line", NEVER "give us your data".
**References:** `clients/superzoo/delivered/2026-07-pilot/` (pilot pitch),
`clients/sportisimo/delivered/decks/` (pipeline chain deck).

## Structure

1. Hook: "[Přední český řetězec] s námi měří prodejnu jako e-shop — 92 % nákupů spárováno."
2. What they get OPERATIONALLY (stage 3): monthly action briefs — 3 changes + expected CZK
   + we measure the impact ourselves ("neděláme doporučení, děláme měřené experimenty")
3. What they get FINANCIALLY: their audience revenue — brands pay, retailer keeps majority,
   full veto, trading-data firewall stated explicitly
4. Proof: case study + (if available) brand LOIs = demand already exists
5. Control & privacy page: veto on every output, k>=25, can cancel anytime
6. The ask (per deal stage in facts.yaml): mandate letter (60 days, zero risk) — never
   open with the full rights license
7. Next step: one small concrete date

## Rules
- Never show brand-facing pricing (internal_only). Never name other retailers without
  their `can_name_publicly`. The whole deck must pass the forwardability litmus.
