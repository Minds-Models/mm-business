# Brand sales deck — spec

**Audience:** brand trade/shopper marketing or insights (JTI/PMI/BAT/Asahi model).
Language: CZ (EN for global HQ). **Story:** MEASUREMENT first, personas second.
**References:** `clients/eurooil/delivered/tobacco-decks/` (JTI/PMI/BAT + generic, CZ+EN),
`decks/mindsmodels-tobacco-pitch.html`.

## Structure

1. Hook: closed-loop in-store measurement — which segment bought which SKU, at the shelf
   (92% match methodology proof)
2. The gap they live with: syndicated data = what sold, weeks later, no who
3. Category-specific value: [category] segments, daypart, basket — with the category's
   unique attribute if any (tobacco: smoking_type = cigarette/pouch/vape/cigar)
4. Offer ladder: study (150–500k CZK, 4 weeks, not a seat) → founding seat (€40–60k, floor €40k, 3 per category) → list T1 from €75k → channel subscription at density
5. Proof & privacy: pilot metrics + GDPR-native page
6. The ask: study pre-order / contingent LOI (form ready) + budget-owner question

## Rules
- POS labels: real (GymBeam/SuperZoo) vs modeled (EuroOil) — never blur.
- Pre-mandate: methodology only, no retailer category aggregates.
- "Age estimation with error bands" language for tobacco. No exclusivity offered.
- Big brands (P&G-tier): never lead with personas — they have them; lead with closed-loop.
