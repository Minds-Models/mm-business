Appendix 4 of 4 · sensitivity cases run against the live model
Appendix · What breaks it

The four questions worth asking, answered before you ask them.

What if the Series A never happens?

The United States never opens and 2031 ARR lands near half the base case. The company still never runs out of cash. The Series A does not buy survival, it buys the American half of the exit.

What if churn is three times the assumption?

Tested at 15% and 25% against a modelled 8%. The 25% case is what moved the Series A from €3.5M to €4.0M. At that size every one of the nine tested cases stays funded, with the low point between €223k and €298k of cash.

Is profitability too far out?

EBITDA turns positive in month 57 and stays positive, at €20.3M of ARR on €6.4M of total capital. The full-year 2031 margin of 8.7% averages six loss-making months with six profitable ones. December run-rate is 17.9%.

What is genuinely unproven?

Rights breadth. Everything above assumes we sign perpetual aggregate mandates chain by chain, and that is the constraint this round exists to remove. The exit ladder prices the case where we do not, and it still returns 5.3×.
