Why this is the moment
Models stopped being the moat. Every fund now asks what a competitor could not copy with more money. Ours is a signature per retail chain, and signatures do not move faster for capital.
Real-world data became the scarce input. The public corpus is used up. 47.4bn USD went into physical AI in the first half of 2026, chasing behavior nobody ever recorded.
The rights are still unclaimed. A chain grants its data mandate once. In our markets nobody has asked yet, and the first signature closes the category.
