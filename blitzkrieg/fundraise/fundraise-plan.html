The VC Case: Why We Are a €100M+ Exit
  • The Target: €100-150M strategic acquisition by 2031 (10-16x return for this €6M cap Seed round).
  • The Buyer: NielsenIQ, Circana, Kantar, or Retail Media networks who structurally lack the "who bought" layer in physical retail.
  • The Valuation Arbitrage: We are building a measurement currency (trades at 8-12x ARR), not a mixed SaaS tool (trades at 5x). We only need €8-12.5M ARR by 2031 to hit our €100-150M exit target.
  • The Defensible Moat: We secure perpetual aggregate data rights across multiple retailers. An acquirer cannot recreate this cross-retailer network overnight; they must buy us or face disruption.
  • The Win-Win Wedge: We don't buy data from retailers (which they refuse). We build them their own dunnhumby-style profit center. They monetize their audience, we take the infra margin and the aggregate rights.
The Pivot Narrative: Re-engaging Warm Leads

How to frame the shift to investors who passed on the old model:

  • Acknowledge the Shift: "Since we last spoke, we've made a major strategic shift. We realized the personalization / SaaS model capped our ceiling. We've pivoted to become a measurement currency."
  • The Revenue Unlock: "We stopped trying to sell data back to retailers (which they hate because it costs them leverage) and started building their own Retail Media networks for them. The retailers keep the majority, but we keep the aggregate data rights."
  • The Valuation Multiplier: "This shifts our trajectory from a mixed SaaS (5x multiple) to an independent measurement currency (8-12x multiple). It’s the exact model that made NielsenIQ a $4B company."
  • The Catalyst: "We are raising this €500k to execute on our first 3 major retailer signatures that prove this cross-retailer moat and to kickstart our European and grocery expansion."
Pitch Deck Checklist: The Currency Narrative

Core 10-12 slides needed to raise at the €6M cap:

  • 1. The Vision: Building the missing "Who Bought" layer for physical retail.
  • 2. The Problem: Brands have endless budget for measurement, but current POS data only shows "What Sold".
  • 3. The Solution & Product: Visual data infrastructure providing in-store, closed-loop measurement (and personas for mid-tier brands).
  • 4. The Win-Win Business Model & Brand Pricing: How retailers monetize their audience (Retailer-as-Monetizer) and exactly how much brands pay (e.g., €5k-10k pilots, €30k+ subscriptions). We take an infrastructure margin.
  • 5. The Moat & Valuation Asset: We retain perpetual aggregate data rights across all retailers. This cross-retailer aggregate is what acquirers will pay a premium (8-12x) for.
  • 6. Traction & Pipeline: SuperZoo DPA, EuroOil timeline, and the €250k-500k brand LOIs (JTI/PMI) proving willingness to pay.
  • 7. The €100-150M Exit Path: Reverse-engineered math showing we only need €8-12.5M ARR to achieve a €100-150M exit by 2031, returning 10-16x on this round.
  • 8. Competition / The Acquirer Matrix: Why NielsenIQ, Circana, or Nectar360 will buy us rather than compete with us.
  • 9. Team: Why we are the only team that can pull off this complex B2B/Retailer/Brand triangle.
  • 10. The Ask: €500k at a €6M pre-money cap to lock the first 3 retail mandates, launch the cross-retailer aggregate v1, and seed European/grocery expansion.
