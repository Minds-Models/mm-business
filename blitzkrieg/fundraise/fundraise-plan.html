The Rolling Close Strategy (Angels -> VCs)

How we create momentum and force VC term sheets without lowering our €6M cap:

  • The Unfair Advantage (We are already funded): We are not starting from zero. We have €80k committed from an existing angel (at a €3.5M cap) who wants to follow on, plus his network. We also have €66k zero-equity from CzechInvest. We pitch from a position of strength.
  • Defending the €6M Cap (The Ownership Play): VCs typically want 10-15% ownership. At a €6M pre-money cap, a €300k ticket only buys ~4.6%. If a Tier-1 VC pushes back on the €6M cap because they need more ownership, we do not lower the cap. Instead, we expand the round. We tell them: "The cap is €6M, but if you want 10-12%, you can write a €750k - €1M ticket." We leverage their ownership targets to extract more capital, not a lower valuation.
  • Phase 1: The Insider Syndicate (Weeks 1-3). Before pushing VCs, secure the remaining €150k-€200k from the existing angel and his network. Pitch: "We have non-dilutive capital, we have €80k committed, and we are locking in early commitments from our inner circle to fund initial hardware deployment. VC outreach begins next month at a €6M cap. Get in now."
  • Phase 2: The VC Leverage (Weeks 4-7). Pitch VCs concurrently with the angel momentum. Pitch: "We are raising €500k. We already have €150k+ committed from insiders, plus €66k from CzechInvest, covering our immediate runway. We are looking for a €300k Lead to close out the round at €6M."
  • Phase 3: The Valuation Bump (Weeks 8-10). Once EuroOil is signed (proving the cross-retailer moat), trigger FOMO. Pitch: "EuroOil is signed. Our model is proven. We are taking a Lead at €6M, but the round closes Oct 16th. Any remaining allocation will re-rate to €8M."
The Pivot Narrative: Re-engaging Warm Leads

How to frame the shift to investors who passed on the old model:

  • Acknowledge the Shift: "Since we last spoke, we've made a major strategic shift. We realized the personalization / SaaS model capped our ceiling. We've pivoted to become a measurement currency."
  • The Revenue Unlock: "We stopped trying to sell data back to retailers (which they hate because it costs them leverage) and started building their own Retail Media networks for them. The retailer gets a revenue share (max 40%), but we keep the aggregate data rights."
  • The Valuation Multiplier (NIQ vs. AdTech): "NielsenIQ operates at a 1-4x multiple because they are a heavy-headcount, low-margin legacy agency. We play the role of Nielsen in physical retail, but with the unit economics of a modern API-first data platform. Therefore, our target exit multiple aligns with modern measurement currencies and AdTech infrastructure (e.g., DoubleVerify, Integral Ad Science (IAS), LiveRamp, Criteo), which historically trade at 8-15x forward revenue."
  • The Catalyst: "We are raising this €500k to execute on our first 3 major retailer signatures that prove this cross-retailer moat and to kickstart our European and grocery expansion."
Pitch Deck Checklist: The Currency Narrative

Core 10-12 slides needed to raise at the €6M cap:

  • 1. The Vision: Building the missing "Who Bought" layer for physical retail.
  • 2. The Problem: Brands have endless budget for measurement, but current POS data only shows "What Sold".
  • 3. The Solution & Product: Visual data infrastructure providing in-store, closed-loop measurement (and personas for mid-tier brands).
  • 4. The Win-Win Business Model & Brand Pricing: How retailers monetize their audience (Retailer-as-Monetizer) and exactly how much brands pay (e.g., €5k-10k pilots, €30k+ subscriptions). We take an infrastructure margin.
  • 5. The Moat & Valuation Asset: We retain perpetual aggregate data rights across all retailers. This cross-retailer aggregate is what acquirers will pay a premium (8-12x) for.
  • 6. Traction & Pipeline: SuperZoo DPA, EuroOil timeline, and the €250k-500k brand LOIs (JTI/PMI) proving willingness to pay.
  • 7. The €100-150M Exit Path: Reverse-engineered math showing we only need €8-12.5M ARR to achieve a €100-150M exit by 2031, returning 10-16x on this round.
  • 8. Competition & Valuation Peers: The Acquirer Matrix (why NIQ/Circana must buy us) and The Valuation Matrix (why we trade like DoubleVerify, LiveRamp, or IAS at 8-15x, not like legacy research agencies).
  • 9. Team: Why we are the only team that can pull off this complex B2B/Retailer/Brand triangle.
  • 10. The Ask: €500k at a €6M pre-money cap to lock the first 3 retail mandates, launch the cross-retailer aggregate v1, and seed European/grocery expansion.
