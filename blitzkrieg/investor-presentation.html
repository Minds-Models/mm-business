Core Axioms of the Strategy
  • Data Value is VC-Backable: Our core value proposition and exit multiple (8-12x) are driven by the cross-retailer aggregate and syndicated channel reads.
  • Moving Away from Hardware: Own-hardware personalization is not VC-backable. We are pivoting purely into a data business model.
  • Revenue Share Clarity: We keep 100% of the cross-retailer aggregate and syndicated channel reads. Retailer revenue sharing (max 40%) applies only to retailer-attributed audience/Retail Media Network (RMN) products.
