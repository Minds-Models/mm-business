1.4Paper batch to counsel

O⏱ 4h
Prerequisite
Prereq: commercial terms decided by the founders before counsel touches it (price band, escrow trigger, term, cap on founding seats).
Steps
  1. Draft the commercial substance yourselves in plain language: (a) 2026 study invoice, (b) 2027 subscription order w/ density escrow, (c) LOI ("subject to retailer data agreement", 3-month early-access consideration), (d) conditional 2027 order (activates at N stores / 2 chains).
  2. Define the escrow precisely: what density (instrumented stores in that category), by when (Q2-27), measured how, remedy (refund or credit — pick one), who declares it.
  3. Counsel dresses the drafts; they do not invent them.
Tip
Counsel drafting from a blank page costs ~3× and takes ~2 weeks. Drafting from your bullet list takes days.
Tip
The escrow is the trust device — it must be legible in one read. If a brand's lawyer needs 20 minutes to understand the remedy, it stops being reassuring.
Tip
Ask counsel for a Czech and an English version of each; CZ subsidiaries often need CZ for internal approval even when the conversation is in English.
Done when
Done when: four documents at counsel with a delivery date ≤5 Sep.

          3.5LOI volume — scarce, never exclusive

          J + O⏱ 3h
          Prerequisite
          Prereq — doctrine check: the note asked for an "exclusive LOI." Category or brand exclusivity is banned (canon; ruling C3 reconfirmed). The compliant scarcity is real and stronger: the LOI reserves one of three founding seats in the category and grants the 3-month early-access window on custom cuts. "Exclusive" only ever means the founding allocation is limited — never that a brand locks out its competitors.
          Steps
          1. The LOI is the default next step of every methodology meeting: "it costs you nothing, it's subject to the retailer agreement, and it reserves your founding seat." Signable on a phone (DocuSign, one page).
          2. Cohort effect: brand #2 in a category hears that seat #1 is reserved — the true sentence that produces the second signature.
          3. Every LOI signer is asked for one intro to a peer brand in another category.
          4. The free teaser-index recipients (~20) get the LOI link with the index.
          5. Target: ≥6 signed LOIs by 30 Sep, ≥2 per active category.
          Tip
          LOIs are free for brands, so the constraint on volume is our meeting capacity, not their willingness — which is exactly why cohort day and the teaser index exist as scaling instruments.
          Tip
          Never let an LOI drift toward implied exclusivity in its wording; if a brand asks for it, the scripted answer is the founding-seat cap plus the early-access window, and nothing more.
          Done when
          Done when: ≥6 LOIs signed; scarcity language in every LOI matches the founding-seat rule.
