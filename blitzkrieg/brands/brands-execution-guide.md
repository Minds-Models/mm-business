# WEEK 1 · Aug 24–30 — Maps, paper, and the tobacco forcing

## 1.1 Budget-owner map — 15+ named humans · J ⏱6h

⛔ **Prereq:** none. Start here; everything else prices off it.

**Steps**
1. Fix the target list per category — **tobacco:** PMI CZ, JTI CZ, BAT CZ, Philip Morris Slovakia ·
   **pet:** Nestlé Purina CZ, Mars/Royal Canin CZ, Vafo/Brit + 3 mid-tier (🔍 below) ·
   **drugstore/HPC:** P&G CZ, Henkel CZ, L'Oréal CZ, Unilever CZ, Beiersdorf CZ ·
   **beverages:** Coca-Cola HBC CZ, Kofola, Mattoni, Red Bull CZ, Plzeňský Prazdroj.
2. For each company find **three** named people, not one:
   (a) **insights** — the methodology gatekeeper · (b) **trade marketing / category / key account** —
   usually holds the money · (c) **brand or marketing manager** — the campaign use-case.
3. For each person log: name · exact title · LinkedIn URL · which of the three use-cases they own ·
   **warm path** (who we know who knows them: Moris, advisor, angels, Šepek network, ex-colleagues).
4. Put it in the CRM as Person records linked to a Company, with a "budget hypothesis" field.

💡 **Use their internal vocabulary** — P&G calls insights **CMK** (Consumer & Market Knowledge);
Nestlé uses *Market Intelligence*; others *Consumer Insights*. Writing "CMK" to a P&G person signals
insider in one word.
💡 **Trade marketing pays, insights vetoes.** You need both: trade to fund it, insights not to block
it on method. Never approach only one.
💡 **Hunt the ex-incumbent people.** Anyone who moved from NIQ/GfK/Kantar into a brand understands
syndicated data instantly and converts fastest. LinkedIn: past company = NielsenIQ, current = brand.
💡 A CZ title often has no CZ budget — regional clusters hold the pen. Never assume; ask explicitly
(see 2.3).

🔍 **Research — mid-tier pet brands (⏱2h).** *What:* the 3–5 real third-party pet brands with
meaningful CZ presence and a marketing budget (candidates to verify: Brit/Vafo, Josera, Tetra,
Dibaq, Calibra, Bozita). *Where:* SuperZoo/Dino e-shop brand filter, pet-trade press, ARES for
CZ entities. *Do what with it:* they are the realistic pet founding-seat buyers if Purina/Mars turn
out thin at SuperZoo (see 1.2) — this list is the fallback that keeps the pet slot alive.

✅ **Done when:** 15+ named people in CRM, each with role, use-case and a warm path (or an explicit
"cold" flag).

## 1.2 SuperZoo third-party brand share (ruling C7) · J or D ⏱3h

⛔ **Prereq:** none.

**Steps**
1. Pull the assortment from the Super zoo e-shop: category tree → brand filter → SKU counts.
2. Split own-brand vs third-party by sub-category (dry food, wet food, treats, accessories, litter).
3. If a store visit is possible, photograph shelf facings in the two biggest categories — **facings
   beat SKU counts** as a signal of who really matters commercially.
4. Compute third-party share and write a three-line verdict.

💡 **The decision this drives:** if third-party share is thin, Purina and Mars will not buy a
SuperZoo-based read at any price — the pet slot then re-aims at the mid-tier list (1.1 🔍) *and* at
SuperZoo's own-brand team as a brand-like buyer (the principled D1 exception). Knowing this in
week 1 instead of October is worth days of wasted pitching.

✅ **Done when:** one-page verdict with a number, filed to `clients/superzoo/facts.yaml` and the
pet-seat plan adjusted.

## 1.4 Paper batch to counsel · O ⏱4h

⛔ **Prereq:** commercial terms decided by the founders **before** counsel touches it (price band,
escrow trigger, term, cap on founding seats).

**Steps**
1. Draft the commercial substance yourselves in plain language: (a) **2026 study invoice**,
   (b) **2027 subscription order** w/ density escrow, (c) **LOI** ("subject to retailer data
   agreement", 3-month early-access consideration), (d) **conditional 2027 order** (activates at
   N stores / 2 chains).
2. Define the escrow precisely: *what* density (instrumented stores in that category), *by when*
   (Q2-27), *measured how*, *remedy* (refund or credit — pick one), *who declares it*.
3. Counsel dresses the drafts; they do not invent them.

💡 Counsel drafting from a blank page costs ~3× and takes ~2 weeks. Drafting from your bullet list
takes days.
💡 The escrow is the trust device — it must be **legible in one read**. If a brand's lawyer needs
20 minutes to understand the remedy, it stops being reassuring.
💡 Ask counsel for a **Czech and an English** version of each; CZ subsidiaries often need CZ for
internal approval even when the conversation is in English.

✅ **Done when:** four documents at counsel with a delivery date ≤5 Sep.

# WEEK 2 · Aug 31–Sep 6 — Teasers and the methodology funnel

## 2.1 Three teaser cards — the "how did you get this?" opener · D + J ⏱6h

⛔ **Prereq:** 1.5 permission · legal-source discipline: **GymBeam, sportstore, pooled brand-worn
only.** SuperZoo/EuroOil pilot data is off-limits until that retailer's mandate is signed.

**Steps**
1. D mines the existing corpus for findings that are **counterintuitive, actionable, and
   category-relevant** — one per target category where possible.
2. Build each card: one visual · one sentence of finding · one "so what" line for that brand's
   use-case.
3. Legal pass: k≥25, no un-mandated chain named, nothing modeled presented as measured.

💡 **Counterintuitive beats impressive.** "We capture 88 attributes" impresses nobody. *"Your
premium buyer is on average 12 years older than your campaign targets"* gets a meeting.
💡 The best teaser **contradicts something the brand believes about its own shopper** — that is what
produces the "how do you know that?" reaction we are engineering for.
💡 **One card per meeting**, chosen for that audience. Three exist for coverage, not to be shown
together.
💡 Put the method in the footnote, not the headline — insights people will check it, everyone else
will skip it, and both behaviours are fine.

🔍 **Research — teaser mining (D, ⏱4h, inside the 6h).** *What:* 3 defensible findings from held
data. *Where:* GymBeam POS-matched set, sportstore report, pooled brand-worn observations.
*Do what with it:* each becomes a card; **log the exact query/sample** behind each so we can defend
it live when someone asks "on what n?"

✅ **Done when:** 3 cards exist, each with its n and method documented on the back.

## 2.2 Book 8–10 methodology meetings · J ⏱8h

⛔ **Prereq:** 1.1 map · 2.1 cards.

**Steps**
1. Sequence: warm paths first (advisor/Moris/angel intros), cold second.
2. Cold email formula: subject *"in-store shopper data — methodology question"* · one line on who
   we are · the teaser card attached · **ask for their opinion on the method** · 20 minutes.
3. Log every send with a dated follow-up (+4 working days, then +7).

💡 **Asking an insights manager for their opinion is the highest-converting cold open in this
market.** It is flattering, genuinely useful to us, and it establishes peer posture instead of
vendor posture.
💡 Never write "we'd like to present our solution." That is a vendor email and it dies.
💡 Batch sends Tue–Thu mornings; CZ/SK corporate inboxes are worst on Mondays and Fridays.
💡 If a brand has an agency of record, do **not** route through the agency yet — go direct, and
bring the agency in later as an ally (see 3.1 💡).

✅ **Done when:** 8–10 meetings booked in the calendar (not "interested" — booked).

## 2.3 R2 — subsidiary spend autonomy · J + A ⏱3h

**Steps** Build the autonomy map: which CZ/SK subsidiaries can sign ~€35k locally vs which escalate
to a regional cluster. Sources: the retained advisor, LinkedIn org shape, and one direct question
asked casually at the end of every methodology call:
> *"If you wanted to buy a €35k data study just for the Czech market, would that be your signature
> or would it go to the region?"*

💡 This may be **the real ICP filter — above category.** A mid-tier brand with local autonomy will
close faster than a giant with a regional approval chain, regardless of category fit.
💡 Ask it at the *end*, conversationally. Asked early it reads as qualification and closes people up.

✅ **Done when:** autonomy flag (local / regional / unknown) on every CRM company record.

# WEEK 3 · Sep 7–13 — Discovery on record

## 3.1 Run the first methodology meetings · J ⏱10h

⛔ **Prereq:** 2.2 bookings.

**Steps** — every meeting logs **three answers**, no exceptions:
1. *What do you buy today for this channel, and what can't it tell you?*
2. *At what coverage would this augment or replace that line?* (their density requirement — this
   prices our ladder)
3. *Who owns that budget, and when do you plan next year's data & measurement spend?* (R3 — the
   renewal date **is** that account's calendar)

💡 **Do not pitch.** The moment you pitch you lose both the methodology-only legal protection and
the peer posture. If they ask for a proposal, say: *"I'd rather show you the method first — the
commercial part only makes sense once you've told me what coverage you'd need."*
💡 Write the three answers into the CRM **within 30 minutes** of the call. Memory decays and these
answers are the pricing input for the entire pillar.
💡 Add a fourth question when the room is warm: *"what would make this a must-have rather than a
nice-to-have?"* — P&G already told us "complementary" is the price cap; this question finds the
path over it.
💡 **The agency-of-record play:** if they mention their agency, ask *"would it help if we briefed
them alongside you?"* — that converts a potential blocker into a co-owner.

✅ **Done when:** 4+ meetings held, all three answers logged for each.

## 3.2 P&G-style free mini-read · D ⏱10h (Dima's allocation)

⛔ **Prereq:** D2 calibration work must not slip — this is the one thing allowed to share Dima's
week, nothing else.

**Steps**
1. Scope: one category, one quarter, **incumbent-shaped Excel** — same tab structure, same period
   columns, same metric naming as the syndicated report it sits beside.
2. Build from legally usable data only (same rule as 2.1).
3. Deliver with a **€0 invoice**, not just a file.

💡 **Invoice it at €0 anyway.** A zero-value PO creates a **vendor record** in their system — which
is 80% of next year's procurement battle already won, and it is the direct answer to P&G's stated
"the hurdle is the budget bucket."
💡 Matching the incumbent's file shape means it slots into an existing workflow with zero learning
cost. Do not improve their format; imitate it.

🔍 **Research — R4, Q4 budget mechanics (J + A, ⏱2h).** *What:* how unspent 2026 trade/research
budget can still be committed in Nov–Dec (PO cut this year, delivery next? prepayment? phased
invoice?). *Where:* the advisor, one friendly brand finance contact, one procurement person.
*Do what with it:* it decides whether the two paying seats close on **2026** money (faster) or must
wait for 2027 budgets — i.e. whether the goal is reachable inside the window at all.

✅ **Done when:** read delivered + €0 invoice raised + vendor record confirmed.

# WEEK 7 · Oct 5–11 — Cohort day: the highest-leverage event in the pillar

## 7.1 Run cohort day #1 · J + O ⏱8h (+prep)

⛔ **Prereqs:** 6.2 confirmed attendees · signed paper physically in the room · seat-pricing memo
(4.2) decided · escrow one-pager already sent.

**Run of show (half day)**
1. **00:00–00:30 — Method.** How the data is produced, what it can and cannot say, the honest nulls,
   the self-benchmark kit. No selling.
2. **00:30–01:00 — The read.** The actual category finding on their category. This is where the
   room changes temperature.
3. **01:00–01:15 — The offer.** Three founding seats, the discount, the escrow, the density
   milestones, no exclusivity — stated once, plainly, with the paper visible.
4. **01:15–end — 1:1 slots.** 20 minutes per brand, back to back, same day.

💡 **Never let them negotiate in front of each other.** Present the offer to the room; close in the
1:1s. Public negotiation collapses your price to the most skeptical person present.
💡 **The scarcity must be real** — 3 seats, and you must be willing to say "that one's taken" later.
Fake scarcity in a small market gets discovered and it poisons the category.
💡 Put the escrow on the wall: *"if density misses, you get refunded."* It converts the biggest
objection (buying data that doesn't fully exist yet) into evidence of confidence.
💡 Have the **retailer say a sentence** if they're co-hosting. One line from the chain is worth ten
of your slides.
💡 Book the 1:1s **before** the day, not after — "I've got a slot at 14:20, shall I keep it for you?"
💡 Assign O to take notes in every 1:1 so J can stay in the conversation. Objections logged verbatim
become next week's collateral.

**Immediately after (same day):** send each attendee their 1:1 summary + the paper + a dated next
step. Cohort momentum decays within 48 hours.

🔍 **Research — pre-day dossier per attendee (J, ⏱3h).** *What:* their current data spend, their
category share direction, one recent campaign or NPD launch. *Where:* their annual report, trade
press, the methodology-meeting notes, the advisor. *Do what with it:* one personalised "so what"
line per brand in the 1:1 — the difference between a generic room and a room where each person
hears their own problem named.

✅ **Done when:** ≥2 verbal seat commitments + every attendee has a dated next step.
