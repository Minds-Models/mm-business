# PART A: COUNSEL BRIEF

## What this document does commercially

It is the seat. Everything in the brands pillar is counted from it: D17 defines a seat as
≥€40k contracted, and the blitzkrieg goal of ≥€80k is two of these. It has to do three jobs at
once: commit a 2027 budget during the October to November planning lock, survive a
compliance-heavy legal review quickly, and make a brand comfortable buying a measurement product
whose underlying estate does not exist yet.

## The four devices, in order of importance

**1. The density escrow (art. 7).** This is the trust device and the reason the document works
at all. A brand is being asked to fund a read against an estate of one live store. The escrow
says: if we have not instrumented 30 rights-clean outlets carrying your set by 30 June 2027, the
second invoice is never issued and the first is refunded in full.

The clause ends with a sentence stating that it is subject to nothing further. **That sentence is
deliberate and it is aimed at you.** Every instinct a commercial lawyer has will be to qualify
this: reasonable endeavours, force majeure, a notice requirement, a cure period, a cap. Each of
those makes the clause safer for us and worthless to the brand. Its entire commercial value is
that a category manager reads it once and believes it.

**2. Activation-time density confirmation (art. 6).** We are signing a forecast. The measured
sessions rate and receipt-link yield do not land until 11 October (RATE-1), and EuroOil stations
are **tenant-operated**, so installation may need per-tenant consent rather than one central
signature (`clients/eurooil/facts.yaml` warns in terms: *verify before any escrow language is
signed*). So at activation we hand the brand a written density statement, and if it shows fewer
than 30 outlets forecast, they may take a revised threshold or walk at no cost. We would rather
lose a deal at activation than pay a refund in July.

**3. Scope, in Schedule 1 (art. 2).** The subscription is to one channel × one competitive set ×
one chain. This is not housekeeping. The renewal price is held **for the read they bought**, so
if the scope definition is loosened, a tobacco brand holding a 1.5M CZK renewal could claim the
8M CZK T2 channel currency at that price. That is roughly €265k per brand of the largest block in
the revenue plan. **Tighten the scope definition, never loosen it.**

**4. The cap and the anti-subdivision promise (art. 5).** Three subscribers per competitive set,
and we promise not to subdivide a named set during the term to sell a fourth. Without the second
half the cap is marketing. With it, it is a term of the contract, which is what lets us use
scarcity honestly (C3). There is **no exclusivity**, and art. 5.3 says so out loud so nobody has
that conversation in month four.

## Questions for counsel

- Is a standalone order enough, or does Czech law want a framework agreement with orders under it?
  Prefer the shortest instrument a category manager can sign without escalating.
- Confirm the refund mechanics in art. 7 (credit note, VAT, revenue recognition on our side).
- Confirm art. 8 (rights failure) does not accidentally give the brand a termination right that
  survives a successful substitution.
- **Tobacco:** confirm nothing here constitutes tobacco advertising, promotion or sponsorship.
  The governing statute is **zákon č. 40/1995 Sb., o regulaci reklamy (§ 3)**; zák. č. 65/2017 Sb.
  is relevant only secondarily (sales and point-of-sale restrictions). We sell measurement to the
  brand; we communicate nothing to consumers. A one-paragraph written confirmation from you is
  itself a sales document on this track.
- **Competition law:** the same read is sold to up to three competing houses (art. 5). Confirm the
  syndicated model complies with the rules on information exchange between competitors
  (§ 3 zák. č. 143/2001 Sb. / Art. 101 TFEU): aggregated, anonymised, historic shopper data from an
  independent third party; no prices, no volumes, no forward-looking conduct. Retail panels of the
  Nielsen type are the established precedent. A tobacco legal team will ask; have the answer ready.
- Art. 2.4 (lapse if the Data Agreement never comes into force) mirrors the study order's long
  stop. Confirm the lapse leaves no residual liability on either side.

## Do not change

- **Art. 7.4** ("subject to nothing further"). Leave the sentence in.
- **Art. 5.3** (not exclusive). Do not delete it as redundant. It is the point.
- **Art. 10.2** (no warranty of end-to-end anonymity). Never warrant this in a contract.
- **Art. 11** (renewal). No indexation, no escalation clause, no CPI link. The named number is
  the device. "Founding members never pay list for the read they bought."
- **Length.** If this document grows past what fits in one sitting, it stops being signable in
  the window that matters, which closes in November.
