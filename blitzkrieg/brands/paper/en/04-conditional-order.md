## 1. Subject

1.1 The Customer orders an annual category read subscription on the terms set out in **Annex A**
(Annual Subscription Order: Founding Seat), within the scope defined in **Schedule 1**
(channel × Competitive Set).

1.2 The terms of Annex A apply in full, subject to the variations in articles 2, 3, 5 and 6 of
this Order.

## 2. Condition precedent

2.1 This Order **takes effect automatically** on the first day of the calendar month following the
date on which **at least two (2) Chains** are instrumented in the Competitive Set ("Activation").

2.2 A Chain is treated as **instrumented** where the Provider holds a data agreement in force in
respect of it and at least **ten (10) Rights-Clean Outlets** carrying the Competitive Set.

2.3 The Provider will notify the Customer that the condition has been met within **5 business
days**. The notice is informational; the Order takes effect under art. 2.1 regardless of it.

2.4 The Customer may at any time request evidence of the number of instrumented Chains and
Rights-Clean Outlets.

## 3. Price

3.1 The first-year subscription fee is **CZK 1,000,000** excluding VAT (founding price). This fee
is **fixed from the date of signature until Activation**, irrespective of how much time passes
between them, and is not indexed.

3.2 The fee for the second and each subsequent year is **CZK 1,500,000** excluding VAT, under
art. 11 of Annex A.

3.3 If study order No. {{study_order_number}} was signed by 31 December 2026 at the latest, the
full study fee is credited against the first-year fee under art. 9.2 of Annex A.

## 4. Founding seat reservation

4.1 For as long as this Order is valid, the Customer holds **one of three (3) founding seats** in
the Competitive Set.

4.2 **No exclusivity is granted** (art. 5.3 of Annex A applies).

## 5. Density guarantee

5.1 By way of variation to art. 1 of Annex A, the **Density Test Date** is the last day of the
sixth calendar month following Activation.

5.2 Otherwise articles 6 and 7 of Annex A apply unchanged, including the density statement at
Activation and the Customer's right to withdraw at no cost under art. 6.2 of Annex A.

## 6. Long stop

6.1 If Activation has not occurred by **31 December 2027**, this Order lapses automatically.
Neither party owes the other any performance or compensation, and the reservation under art. 4
falls away.

6.2 Article 2.4 of Annex A (lapse where the Data Agreement is not in force by 31 March 2027) does
not apply to this Order; this article applies in its place.

## 7. General

7.1 Governing law: Czech law. Jurisdiction: the courts of Prague.

7.2 This Order exists in Czech and English versions. The version actually signed prevails.
