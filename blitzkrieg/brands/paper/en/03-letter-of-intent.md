the study order (signature by 31 December 2026).

same read. The Provider grants category exclusivity to no subscriber.

b) to **investors and their advisers**, bound by written confidentiality obligations.

This consent does **not** extend to public, press or marketing use, which requires the Interested
Party's separate prior written consent.

The Interested Party may withdraw this consent at any time in writing, with effect for the future.

## 6. Confidentiality *(binding)*

The parties will keep confidential the content of their discussions and the materials exchanged,
for two (2) years. This does not apply to publicly available information or to disclosures under
art. 5.

## 7. Nature of this document

This Letter is **non-binding**, save for articles **5 (naming permission)** and
**6 (confidentiality)**, which are binding. It creates no obligation to enter into a contract and
no obligation to negotiate.

## 8. Validity

This Letter expires on **31 March 2027** unless replaced by an order before that date. Articles 5
and 6 survive expiry.
