# PART A: COUNSEL BRIEF

## What this document does commercially

It converts a 2026 marketing or research budget line into signed paper **before** we hold retailer
rights, and it is half of a two-document close: the study fee credits 100% against the 2027 seat
(document 2), so the brand's planner books both in one decision. We get cash and a reference this
year, ARR at the budget tally.

## The three things that carry the weight

1. **It is an order, not an invoice.** 1.4 calls it a "study invoice", but an invoice commits
   nothing. What commits the 2026 budget at a multinational subsidiary is a signed order against
   which a **purchase order is raised in 2026**. The invoice can land later. Draft so that
   signature plus PO is the committing act.
2. **Delivery is conditional on retailer rights we do not yet hold.** Zero rights instruments are
   signed today. So: delivery runs from the date the retailer data agreement is in force, with a
   long-stop of 31 March 2027, after which the order lapses and neither side owes anything. This
   is honest and it is also the reason a brand can sign it in September.
3. **The fee is not refundable once the study is delivered.** The escrow in document 2 protects
   the subscription, not this. Do not let the escrow language bleed backwards into this document:   a delivered study has been delivered.

## Questions for counsel

- Is this a `smlouva o dílo` (a study as a work) or a service order? We prefer whichever produces
  the shorter instrument that a category manager can sign without escalating to legal.
- Confirm the long-stop lapse leaves no residual liability on either side.
- Confirm the 100% credit into document 2 is drafted so it does not read as a discount that could
  be clawed back if document 2 later terminates under the escrow.

## Do not change

- Do not add an advance payment or deposit. We are an unknown vendor asking a compliance-heavy
  buyer for money against undelivered work. Payment on delivery is what makes this signable.
- Do not add a clause letting us substitute the data source without telling them.
- Do not delete clause 3.4 (what the study is not). It is the anti-overpromise clause and it is
  what keeps us honest when the modeled tobacco decks are in the room. Hard repo rule: modeled POS
  is always labelled as modeled, never presented as measured.

- **Channel:** {{channel}} (e.g. petrol and convenience, Czech Republic)
- **Competitive set:** {{competitive_set}} (e.g. nicotine products)
- **Chain:** {{chain}}

1.2 The Scope is binding. The Provider will neither widen nor narrow it without the Customer's

### 2. Contents of the Study

The Study comprises:

a) **segmentation of channel visitors** (who is in the stores, and when),
b) **purchase behaviour of each segment within the Competitive Set** (what each segment buys,
   basket value),
c) **daypart and day-of-week structure**,
d) **conversion** (visitors to the category versus purchasers),
e) **written interpretation** proportionate to the findings,
f) **one presentation of results** (in person or online, up to 90 minutes).

Format: data tables (XLSX) and a document (PDF).

### 3. Method and its limits

3.1 The Study is built on **anonymous aggregated data** captured in the Chain's stores. The
minimum reported segment size is **25 persons**.

3.2 The Provider performs no facial recognition, creates no biometric templates and does not work
with identities. Age is **estimated, with the error band stated**.

3.3 In performing this Order the Provider processes no personal data on the Customer's behalf.
No separate data processing agreement is therefore required.

3.4 **What the Study is not.** The Study is not a market measurement and not a benchmark. It is
built on one retail chain and is therefore a **channel read**, not a market average. The Provider
does not and will not present comparisons at the level of individual competing brands until at
least two chains in the Competitive Set are instrumented. Any figure that is not measured is
expressly labelled as modelled in every output.
