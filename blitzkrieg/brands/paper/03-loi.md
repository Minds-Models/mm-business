# PART A: COUNSEL BRIEF

## What this document does commercially

It is the only instrument we can sign **before** any retailer rights exist, which makes it the
bridge asset of the whole quarter. Pre-mandate, doctrine allows methodology conversations and
LOIs and nothing else. Its value is not the intent; it is that a signed LOI becomes leverage in
the retailer pillar (a named brand waiting on their signature) and in the fundraise (demand
evidence before revenue). Volume target: ≥6 by 30 September.

## The structure that makes it work

**It is non-binding, except for two clauses that must bind.** Confidentiality, and the naming
permission. If the naming permission is drafted as non-binding along with everything else, the
document is worthless to us: we cannot tell the retailer or an investor who signed it, and those
two audiences are the entire reason it exists.

Confirm this partial-binding structure holds under Czech law. If it does not, the fix is to split
the two binding clauses into a short separate side letter rather than to make the whole document
binding.

## The naming permission is scoped deliberately

We ask to name the Customer **to retailer counterparties, and to investors and their advisers
under written confidentiality**. We do **not** ask for public, press or marketing use.

That is not timidity. The narrow ask is far easier for a brand's legal team to approve, it is
everything the two other pillars actually need, and asking for public use would put a tobacco
company's communications team in the conversation, which would cost weeks. If the Customer strikes
even the narrow permission, the LOI still has value, but flag it: it drops from an asset to a
courtesy.

## What we give in return

A reservation of one of three founding seats until 31 March 2027, and a three-month early-access
window, defined in art. 4.2 as receiving the first Quarterly Read at least three months before it
is offered to any non-founding subscriber.

**Not exclusivity.** C11 is explicit: an "exclusive LOI" conflicts with canon. The compliant
scarcity is the reservation, and it is stronger, because it is real and we can prove it.

## Do not change

- **Do not make the naming permission mutual or reciprocal.** We need theirs; they do not need ours.
- **Do not grant exclusivity**, in any form, including a right of first refusal over the set.
- **Do not turn the indicative budget band into a commitment.** It is intelligence, not an
  obligation, and drafting it as an obligation is what would make a brand refuse to state it.
- **Keep it to one page.** An LOI that needs legal review defeats its own purpose.
