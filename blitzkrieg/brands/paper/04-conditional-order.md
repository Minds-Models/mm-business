# PART A: COUNSEL BRIEF

## What this document does commercially

It is the scripted answer to the single most common brand objection: *"come back when you have
more chains."* Today that sentence ends the conversation and we leave with nothing. This document
converts it into paper: **fine, here is the order that signs itself on the day that happens.**

The trigger is not arbitrary. Two instrumented chains is exactly the point at which a
single-chain "channel read" becomes a legitimate cross-chain claim under our own benchmark
integrity rule. So the activation condition **is** the objection, which is what makes it hard to
refuse without admitting the objection was never the real reason.

## How it is drafted

It **incorporates the founding seat order by reference** rather than restating it. One set of
terms to maintain, one document to negotiate, and it keeps this instrument to two pages. If you
prefer a standalone document, say so, but do not let the two drift apart.

## Three judgment calls to review

**1. It auto-activates.** The founders chose automatic activation over a 30-day confirmation
window. This is the harder ask and it is the clause most likely to be resisted, because the
Customer is binding a 2027 or 2028 budget to a future event outside their control. **Do not
concede a confirmation window without a founder call.** A window turns this from an order into an
option, and an option counts for materially less in fundraise diligence. If they will not sign
without one, that is a decision, not a drafting fix.

**2. "Instrumented chain" carries a minimum (art. 2.2).** Without it, the trigger could fire on a
second chain with one outlet in it, which would be a technicality rather than the thing the
Customer was waiting for. Ten rights-clean outlets per chain is a definitional qualifier, not a
second condition. If the founders want the trigger stripped back to bare chain count for
legibility, this is the line to delete.

**3. It has its own density test date.** The founding seat order tests density on 30 June 2027.
That date is meaningless here, because this order might activate in late 2027 or in 2028. So the
test runs six months after Activation instead. Check the drafting: a stale test date would hand
the Customer an automatic refund the day the order activates.

## Confirm for us

- That automatic activation on a future uncertain event works as an `odkládací podmínka`, and
  that the trigger as drafted is objectively determinable by a third party.
- That the 31 December 2027 long-stop leaves no residual liability once it passes.
- That incorporation by reference of the founding seat order is effective where that order is a
  separate unsigned document annexed to this one.
- That the disapplication of Annex A art. 2.4 in art. 6.2 is clean, so this Order is governed
  solely by its own 31 December 2027 long stop.

## Do not change

- **Do not add a price escalator** between signature and activation. The locked founding price is
  the entire reason a brand signs a document that may not activate for a year.
- **Do not let the seat reservation (art. 4) run without an expiry.** A conditional order holds
  one of only three seats in the set; without the long-stop, a brand parks a slot for free.
- **Label discipline (C5).** A signed conditional order is SIGNED but contingent. It is not active
  contracted ARR and must never appear in a deck or data room as though it were.
