The Sequencing Truth (plan around it, don't fight it)

The dependency that rules this pillar

No brand sees retailer data before that retailer's mandate is signed. Pre-mandate = methodology talks + LOIs ("subject to retailer data agreement") only. The math: Mandate #1 ~mid/late Sep → cohort day ~2 weeks later → first invoices ≤ Oct 31.

If the mandate slips past ~Oct 5, the 2 paying seats slip out of the budget window. Fallback (pre-agreed, no December panic): land the €70k as signed 2027 orders + one 2026 study invoice — the pet study is sellable off data we already hold the day rights exist.

The licensed one-liner (external)

“Syndicated data tells you what sold. We show you who buys it — in the aisle, receipt-verified.” Depth claim: “hundreds of attributes per shopper, zero identities.” Never say “100× better than NIQ” externally — unfalsifiable, and it wakes the incumbent. The self-benchmark kit (receipt-parity + observer tally + label-buy precision) backs every claim.

Risk Analysis & Mitigations

Risk: The rights trap on teasers

The “how did you get this?” door-opener may NOT use SuperZoo/EuroOil pilot data pre-mandate. Mitigation: 3 teaser cards from legal sources only (GymBeam lab, anonymized sportstore, pooled brand-worn / fashion read). SuperZoo teasers get built the day the mandate signs.

Risk: The quiet internal loser

The insights manager who owns the incumbent-data relationship, and the agency of record, can slow-walk us to death. Mitigation: sell THROUGH them — co-design credit, advisory micro-board seat; the read is presented internally as their find.

Risk: Tobacco never materializes

A summer of stalled meetings. Mitigation: three channels at once (Moris letter · PM Slovakia direct · 2 cold letters w/ sportstore report). Kill-test: no meeting with a budget owner by Sep 30 → line cut for 2026, hours reweighted to pet + drugstore.

Risk: The “complementary data” price cap

Brands say “interesting” but won't pay measurement prices. Mitigation: attach to an EXISTING budget line at renewal, founding-seat discount + density escrow (refund printed in bold). Kill-test: zero seats after ≥6 qualified conversations → reprice to studies, land-expand.

Target Brand Profiles

  • Tobacco (JTI, PMI, BAT, PM Slovakia): biggest wallet, buys channel reads already. Gated on the Sep 30 verdict + EuroOil path. PM-SK seat = the cross-border logo.
  • Pet (Purina, Mars/Royal Canin, Vafo/Brit + mid-tier): approached only WITH SuperZoo, post-mandate. Vafo/Brit = first-study candidate (hungriest, personas land). ⚠ W1 task: verify SuperZoo's third-party brand share — if own-brand dominates, the own-brand team becomes the buyer.
  • Drugstore / FMCG lighthouse (P&G-type): free quarterly mini-read in incumbent-shaped Excel, €0 invoice → “add a line at renewal.” Revenue expected Q1-27, never counted earlier.
  • Agencies (media / shopper): R5 explores whether one agency master carries 3+ end-seats.

Cross-Pillar Dependencies

← Retailers: the mandate date

This pillar's calendar IS the mandate date. In return, signed LOIs flow back as EuroOil leverage (“guaranteed money waiting”).

→ Fundraise: traction, honestly labeled

Seats and LOIs enter the deck the day they're SIGNED — never before. Labels: SIGNED / IN NEGOTIATION / TARGET. Retailer-logo FOMO works on brands too: every mandate press-safe mention is brands-pillar ammunition.

← Product: the artifacts

Every meeting opens with an artifact: parity memo (Sep 19), D2 cross-category number (Oct 10), self-benchmark kit, teaser cards, Twin demo.
