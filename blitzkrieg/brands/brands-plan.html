The Segmented Brand Pitch

How to frame the value proposition to different tiers of brands:

  • Giants (P&G, JTI, PMI): Sell Measurement. They already have personas. Do NOT sell them demographics. Sell them "observed, in-store, closed-loop measurement". They have bottomless budgets for measurement and currency-level data.
  • Mid-Tier (GymBeam partners, local FMCG): Sell Insight & Personas. They lack deep market research budgets. Sell them exactly who their customer is, demographic splits, and basket combinations.
The "Land & Expand" Pricing Model

How we hit €70k ARR quickly:

  • Land (The Pilot): €5k–10k for a one-off "Category Read" or "Insight Study". This is fast money and proves immediate value.
  • Expand (The Subscription): €30k+/year for continuous "Measurement Currency Access". The pilot contract MUST contain an automatic transition or clear upsell path to the 12-month subscription. Two €35k subscriptions = €70k ARR.
  • Exploding Offers: Brand LOIs must have a deadline. "Budget is allocated for Q4. If sensors are not deployed by Oct 31, the budget goes elsewhere." This creates massive leverage for the Retailer team.
