# Teaser-card outreach playbook (brands pillar 2.1 + 2.2) · v2 · 25 Sep 2026

Internal. The working document for the first outreach wave: who gets which card, through which
door, with which next step. Copy in Czech lives in [`02-copy-cz.md`](02-copy-cz.md); the scraping
list in [`03-targets.md`](03-targets.md) / [`03-targets.csv`](03-targets.csv); the cards in
[`cards/`](cards/) (gallery: `cards/index.html`).

**Goal of the wave: booked meetings.** Not LOIs, not "interested". A meeting is the unit; the LOI
comes out of the meeting.

---

## 1. What we can and cannot see (read before writing anything)

- **We can:** match a receipt to the visit it came from, so we know **who bought what**. We also
  see **everyone else who was in the store**, so we know who came in and **did not buy** the
  category or the brand. We read what people look like, wear and carry, and when they come.
- **We cannot:** see behaviour at the shelf. No "picked it up and put it back", no "compared two
  packs", no "stood in front of the fixture". **Never claim it**, in a card, a message or a meeting.
- **No retailer is ever named** in anything that goes out. Data is described as *reálná data z
  prodejen v Česku* (all live stores are Czech today; don't write "ČR a SR" until a Slovak store is live).
- **Every number on a card is real.** Cards built on receipts say *pokladní a vizuální data*;
  cards built on camera attributes only say *vizuální data*.

## 2. Three proof types (the landing page's three brand questions)

| # | Landing-page question | What the card proves | Cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Which promotion is free money?"** | Who a promotion actually moved, and who would have bought anyway | `pet-promo-3plus1` (3+1 sold more units to the same people, no new buyers) |
| 2 | **"The shopper who said no"** | Who came in and left without buying, and how they differ from buyers | `mall-who-buys` (adults leave with a purchase 2× as often as teens) |
| 3 | **"A brief written from evidence"** | Who actually buys or carries the brand: age, time, style, next to the rival | `fc-*` (drinks), `tob-*` (tobacco counter), `hpc-makeup-50`, `pet-*` |

Proof 2 at brand level (the non-buyers of *your* brand) needs receipts matched to every visit in the
same window. We can build it per store on request; it is the natural "what we'd show you next".

## 3. Card anatomy

One message, one chart, one button. Nothing else.

1. **Pill**: category · channel (*Energetické nápoje · čerpací stanice*). Never the retailer.
2. **Headline**: one sentence; names their brand and the rival where the data is brand-level.
3. **Sub**: one line saying what the number measures.
4. **Big number + label** (top right).
5. **One chart**: bars, a two-number "versus" scoreboard, or a simple line.
6. **Footer**: Minds & Models wordmark · data line · n.
7. **Button**: *Vyzkoušejte si to živě →* (links to chat.mindsmodels.ai in the PDF; in LinkedIn
   and email the link goes in the message text).

### Formats

| Format | Size | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `sq` | 1080×1080 PNG | LinkedIn DM, WhatsApp, inline in email (display at 540–600 px) |
| `pt` | 1080×1350 PNG (4:5) | LinkedIn feed or DM when a taller card reads better |
| `slide` | 1920×1080 PNG + PDF (clickable button) | Drops into the recipient's own deck; PDF attachment |

### Persona → angle → message CTA

| Persona (ICP) | What the message must do | Angle | First CTA (§5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner / jednatel / GM** | One number about *his* customer nobody in his category has; never contradict "znám svého zákazníka", add who he doesn't see | *"Tohle je zákazník, kterého v sell-outu nevidíte."* | L0 → L2 "přijedu za vámi do [město]" |
| **Trade / shopper marketing** | What his in-store money bought. **Never say "insight" or "výzkum"** | *"Akci platíte lidem, kteří by koupili i tak."* | L1 → L2 |
| **Sales / commercial director** | Category evidence for the annual negotiation (Nov–Dec) | *"Tohle můžete v listopadu položit na stůl."* | L2 |
| **KAM / NAM** | A slide for his next chain meeting. Refuse the toxic cut (his share vs a rival in a named chain) | *"Jeden slide do vaší příští schůzky s řetězcem."* | L2 + `slide` |
| **Brand / marketing manager** | Who the brief targets vs who actually buys | *"Brief míří na X, u pokladny je Y."* | L3 |
| **Insights / CMK** (multinationals) | Peer posture: their opinion on the method | *"Jak byste tohle ověřili vy?"* | L3 |
| **Private-label lead** | Own brand vs the brand next to it | *"Kdo kupuje vlastní značku, a kdo tu vedle."* | L2 |
| **Distributor / importer** | Something for his principal that the principal doesn't have | *"Tohle vaši principálové nemají."* | L2 |
| **Mall: asset / head of retail** | Who comes and who buys, per centre | *"Footfall vám řekne kolik. Tohle kdo."* | L2 |
| **Mall: marketing** | Which audience each weekend/event brings | *"Víkend přivádí jiné lidi."* | L1 |
| **Mall: leasing** | Audience evidence for tenant mix | *"Kdo z návštěvníků reálně nakupuje."* | L2 |

## 5. The CTA ladder (in the message, not on the card)

The card always carries *Vyzkoušejte si to živě →*. The message carries **one** of these, growing
only after a smaller yes:

| Level | Ask (CZ) | Why it works | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L0 · reply** | *„Mám k tomu ještě jedno zjištění o [brand]. Poslat?"* | Yes/no reply, zero cost | Owners, first touch |
| **L1 · question challenge** | *„Pošlete mi jednu otázku o vašich zákaznících, na kterou dnes nemáte odpověď. Odpověď z dat vám pošlu do 48 hodin."* | Reciprocity + curiosity | Trade, mall marketing |
| **L2 · brief your team** | *„Rádi to na 20 minut ukážeme vašemu trade týmu. Bez prezentace, jen data a vaše otázky."* | Makes *them* look good internally; "your team" doesn't sound like "our demo" | Trade, KAM, sales dir., distributor, private label, malls |
| **L3 · opinion** | *„Zajímal by mě váš pohled: sedí to s tím, co vidíte? Kde byste to rozporovali?"* | Peer posture; people like to correct | Insights/CMK, brand managers |

Never: "rádi bychom vám představili naše řešení", "nabídka spolupráce", "demo".

## 6. Doors: which channel carries trust

A ~€40k trust-heavy purchase sold to ~40 people in a small market is a **trust-transfer** sale.

1. **Warm intro, done for the introducer.** We write the forwardable blurb + card; the introducer
   forwards it in 30 seconds. Known paths: Vološin (P&G) → Mattoni, Sarantis, Coty, Tomil ·
   Rajnohová (Nestlé) · Ivančáková (L'Oréal) · Šulc (PMI) · ČEPRO → tobacco majors ·
   Moris/advisor/angels → anyone on the list.
2. **Retailer- or distributor-convened.** At the COOP meeting: *"které 3–5 dodavatele byste chtěli
   mít u datového partnerství jako první?"*. Same ask to ČEPRO (tobacco) and PEAL/JIP.
3. **LinkedIn, done like a person.** Connection note with one hook → DM with the card after accept
   → a **30-second voice note** in Czech → second card.
4. **The research interview** (the "university project" door, made honest): *„Dáváme dohromady
   přehled, jak české značky v roce 2027 rozhodují o in-store rozpočtech. 15 minut, výsledky
   dostanete jako první."* We really publish the summary to participants. Co-brand with a
   university only if the collaboration is real; don't pose as students.
5. **Email.** Used, not relied on. `sq` card inline, brand name in the subject, one CTA.
6. **Phone** for owners and distributors once the card has landed.

**Malls: the ANC ČR conference, 1 Oct 2026, Prague** (Asociace nákupních center ČR); most of the
CZ list attends. Pre-book 10-minute coffees (copy §J). CPI's Pavel Urban leads ANC ČR's
marketing commission, and several targets (Westfield, ECE) already count visitors with cameras,
so pitch **who comes and who buys**, never counting.

## 7. Sequences (by what contact data we have)

Send Tue–Thu, 8:00–10:00. Every step after D0 carries **new** value, never "just checking in".

| Day | A · warm intro | B · LinkedIn only | C · email + LinkedIn | D · + phone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D0 | Introducer forwards blurb + `sq` card | Connection request (≤300 chars, hook, no pitch) | Email with `sq` card inline, L0 or L1 | as C |
| D1–3 | Thank-you reply + L2 | On accept: DM + `sq` card + L0/L1 | LinkedIn connect (no note) | Call: "poslal jsem vám kartu…" |
| D4–5 | Second card + slot proposal | 30 s voice note | Reply in thread with the second card | — |
| D9–10 | Call | Second card + L2 | LinkedIn DM or voice note | Call #2 |
| D14–17 | — | Close the loop: *„Mám to nechat na leden?"* | Break-up email: *„…ozvu se v lednu"* | — |

Stop at the first reply of any kind; a "no" gets a thank-you and a January re-entry date in Attio.

## 8. Triggers: the "why now" line

One sentence at the top of the message; public facts only, never a retailer's internal plan.

| Track | Trigger |
|---|---|
| All brands | 2027 plans and budgets are set Oct–Nov; annual negotiations with the chains run Nov–Dec |
| Tobacco | The switch to smoke-free (heated, pouches, vapes) is reshuffling who buys at the forecourt |
| Energy / soft drinks | The draft under-16 energy-drink restriction (parliamentary working group, 2026) |
| Forecourt | Forecourt shops across networks are being rebuilt around gastro and convenience |
| Drugstore / HPC | 58% of Czech food and drugstore spend moves on promotion (64% for brands) |
| Pet | Private label in pet-specialist stores is around half of revenue |
| Malls | Christmas planning; tenant renewals; "footfall tells you how many, not who" |

## 9. Automation: Attio or Claude?

**Attio is the system of record and the email engine; Claude writes and maintains; LinkedIn and
phone stay human.**

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| Contacts, companies, lists, step status | **Attio** (existing brand lists + a *Shopping centres* list) |
| Email steps (D0, D4, D14) | **Attio Sequences** (check it's on your plan) with the per-person text I write; sent from your mailbox, auto-stop on reply |
| LinkedIn, voice and phone steps | **Attio tasks** I create per person, with due date and exact text |
| Personalisation | **Claude**: card + first line + trigger per person |
| Hygiene | **Claude**, Tue/Thu: read replies, move stages, draft next messages |

This session has no Gmail connector, so drafts can't go straight into your inbox; Attio Sequences
is the simpler route.
