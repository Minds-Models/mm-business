# Shopping-centre targets: Czechia + Slovakia (+ international user list)

*Compiled 2026-09-23 for Minds & Models mall outreach. This is internal research, not a CRM export. The CSV twin is `malls_targets.csv`, one row per company, same IDs.*

**What's here:** 45 CZ/SK targets (35 Czech tier, 10 Slovak second tier) and 10 international operators from the user's list. Czech tier by priority: P1 9, P2 12, P3 14. Slovak tier: P1 3, P2 4, P3 3. 3 rows are CPI group and are flagged *"CPI group — expansion, not a new logo"*.

**Priority rules.** P1: multi-centre owner or manager, CZ decision-making, at least one enclosed centre of regional size. P2: a single big centre, or multi-centre with decisions abroad or small formats. P3: everything else. Open-air retail-park operators are capped at P2 because they have no controlled entrance for a camera. Slovakia is a second tier and is ranked P1–P3 within itself.

**Confidence.** A shared web-search budget ran out partway through, and the research was then cut short on purpose. Every ownership or mandate claim cites a URL. Anything whose best source predates 2025, or that could not be confirmed, is marked **VERIFY**. "OR" links point to the Czech company register (or.justice.cz), "ORSR" to the Slovak one; both were read 2026-09-23.

## Read this first

1. **Don't name CPI in outreach.** `clients/cpi/facts.yaml` has `can_name_publicly: false`. With every other target, use the approved fallback *"přední provozovatel obchodních center v ČR"* and do not name Olympia Mladá Boleslav.
2. **"ACSC" is the wrong association.** acsc.cz is a Brno engineering firm. The industry body is **ANC ČR, Asociace nákupních center ČR** (<https://anccr.cz>). Its board chair is Jakub Skwarlo (URW). Inside ANC ČR we already know people: Pavel Urban (CPI) leads the marketing commission and Petr Brabec (CPI) sits on the executive committee. Pavel Klimeš (CBRE) leads the PropTech commission. Its **conference is on 1 Oct 2026 in Prague**, a week away.
3. **Ownership has moved since the brief was written:**
   - IMMOFINANZ is now **CPI Europe AG** (Mar 2025), 75% CPIPG. STOP SHOP is still CPI: 12 parks in CZ, 15 in SK.
   - **VIVO! Bratislava was sold to WOOD & Co** and renamed Polus. CPI Europe stays as a JV partner until WOOD reaches 100% at the end of 2026.
   - **Palladium belongs to REICO** (Nov 2025), not Union Investment.
   - **Arkády Pankrác belongs to Trigea.**
   - **Aupark Bratislava belongs to WOOD + Tatra AM**; URW has left Slovakia.
   - **Bory Mall belongs to ZFP realitní fond** (Sep 2025).
   - **Avion Brno** went to the Polish owner of bauMax, and Avion Zličín to Pradera.
   - **NEPI's Forum Liberec and Forum Ústí are reportedly for sale** (HN, 2026-09-23).
4. **Some targets already count visitors with cameras.** Westfield Chodov and Černý Most run camera-based counting, and both ECE centres measure anonymously. AC Systems (Xovis sensors) is the main counting incumbent. So the pitch is the **8 audiences and the share of visits ending in a purchase**, not counting.
5. **Timing.** Redstone's founder Richard Morávek died on 2026-09-17, so wait a few weeks before approaching Redstone. CPI's Petr Brabec sits on the board of POLUS a.s. (WOOD), so coordinate any WOOD approach with the CPI relationship.

## Method

The research was split five ways: CPI group; international owners in CZ; Czech funds and retail parks; third-party managers, vendors and events; Slovakia. The user's operator list was then checked lightly (one source each). Sources were company and centre contact pages, annual and half-year reports (CPIPG AR 2025 / H1 2026, URW H1-2026, Klépierre URD 2025, NEPI), the CZ/SK company registers, and trade press (HN, E15, Seznam Zprávy, Deník, SME, Retail News/retailguide, mam.cz, Property Forum). LinkedIn was not scraped and nothing was written to a CRM. The raw helper notes are not kept in the repo.
