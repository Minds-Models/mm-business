# 🏢 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

This page defines the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for Minds & Models' brand-side sales. A prospect must match the ICP criteria before a meeting is booked.

---

## Core ICP definition

**Industry:** Consumer brand with physical distribution in a store we instrument. Three shapes qualify: a brand principal (the manufacturer or its national entity), a retailer's own-brand / private-label unit buying like a brand, and an importer or distributor acting as principal for brands with no local entity.

**Geography:** Czech Republic and Slovakia only. Not the EU. The test is not where the company sells, it is where the signature lives.

**Company size:** Not measured in customers or revenue. Measured by the margin test: EUR 40,000 (CZK ~980,000) against filed net profit. Above roughly 2% of net profit, the account prices at a study (150,000 to 500,000 CZK), not at a seat.

**Estate match:** The brand must have shelf presence in an estate we instrument or have in pipeline (GymBeam, Super zoo, EuroOil forecourt, Teta). A brand with no presence on our estate has nothing to buy. There is no national read to sell.

**Autonomy, and this is the filter that actually decides:** Can this legal entity sign ~EUR 40k without the signature leaving the country? Applied to 43 unique legal entities, that one question left 8. It is not size, it is not category, and it is not enthusiasm in the first meeting.

---

## The qualification sequence

Run these four in order. Each is cheaper than the one after it, and each kills accounts the next one would waste a week on.

**1. Local pen.** Is the person who signs a registered statutory officer resident in CZ or SK? Check ARES / obchodní rejstřík or ORSR. Five minutes. A prokurista is not a statutory officer, and this is the field the whole pillar turns on.

**2. Estate match.** Does this brand sit on a shelf in an estate we instrument? Check the shelf audit, the receipt data or the stockist census. If not, stop here and do not book the meeting.

**3. Margin test.** Is EUR 40k below roughly 2% of filed net profit? Check Sbírka listin, the ČSÚ revenue bands, or the Forbes family-firm ranking. Above that line the offer is a study, not a seat.

**4. Rights status.** Does a signed mandate exist for the estate this read would come from? This one does not disqualify: it decides what may be offered. With no mandate the conversation is methodology-only and the paper is a conditional order or an LOI, never an invoice. Currently the status is "none" almost everywhere.

## Tier system

# Tier 1: Ideal fit (prioritize)

- Local pen, verified in the register, ideally with owner and signer in one person
- Present on an estate we already hold, with real POS behind it
- No rights instrument standing between us and the money
- Margin test clears comfortably at T1 pricing (EUR 75,000 to 80,000)
- Decision cycle measured in weeks: no procurement, no legal department, no vendor portal
- Examples from pipeline: GymBeam s.r.o. (Košice), Plaček Pet Products s.r.o. (Poděbrady), Consumer Brands International s.r.o. / DanCzek Teplice a.s.

# Tier 2: Good fit (pursue actively)

- Local pen confirmed, or a country GM who is personally a registered statutory officer
- Present on an estate we hold or have in pipeline, but the mandate is unsigned
- Margin test clears at the founding floor (EUR 40,000)
- Decision cycle 1 to 6 months, so this is a committed 2027 order, not a 2026 invoice
- Sells on a conditional order or an LOI with the mandate as condition precedent
- Examples: VAFO Praha s.r.o., British American Tobacco (Czech Republic) s.r.o., Kofola ČeskoSlovensko a.s., Coca-Cola HBC Česko a Slovensko s.r.o., Teta drogerie / Solvent ČR s.r.o. (as a private-label buyer, and as the mandate that unlocks the whole drugstore category)

# Tier 3: Opportunistic (qualify carefully)

- Local pen confirmed, but the wallet rests on nothing current: a filing from 2013, 2016 or 2020, or no published revenue figure at all
- Presence on our estate is inferred from channel logic rather than from an audit
- Requires a Sbírka listin pass before any price is named
- Only pursue with founder approval, or on a strong inbound signal
- Examples: NUTREND D.S. a.s., Dermacol a.s., Gabriella Salvete s.r.o., Sarantis Czech Republic s.r.o., Big Shock s.r.o., Mattoni 1873 a.s., Horsefeathers / STIGMA DISTRIBUTION, Alpine Pro, Husky, Hannah

# Disqualification criteria (do NOT pursue)

- **The signature leaves the country.** Registry-settled cases: L'Oréal ČR, Coty ČR, Henkel ČR, Beiersdorf, Mars Czech, whose statutory officers are resident in Poland, Warsaw or Vienna. Hub-settled cases: Colgate-Palmolive ČR (Warsaw CEE Hub), Procter & Gamble CZ (Bucharest Intelligence Hub, 20 years old), Haleon Czech Republic (RHCS cluster since July 2025), Farmina Czech Republic (capitalised at the statutory minimum CZK 20,000, a rep-office signal).
- **No estate.** The brand's volume sits in Albert, Kaufland, Lidl, Billa, Tesco or Penny, where we hold nothing. This kills the general beverages track and the national drugstore read.
- **The margin test fails.** EUR 40k above roughly 2% of filed net profit. Imperial Brands CR, at 2.0% of a net profit down 62% to CZK 49.4m, is the worked example.
- **The purchase is conditional on the toxic cut:** their share against a named competitor inside a named chain. We refuse it, and refusing it in minute three is what makes everything else believable.
- **Czech ownership but a central merchandising budget.** Ownership nationality is not the filter, the budget's location is. The Sportisimo private-label cluster is 100% Czech-owned and regional in effect.
- **An IT conversation is required to start.** Against an eleven-week clock, any account that needs client IT capacity or master-data readiness is a 2027 account.

## Why these criteria matter for Minds & Models

**Autonomy, not scale.** The revenue model is a founding seat at a EUR 40,000 floor, then T1 at EUR 75,000 to 80,000, entered through a study at 150,000 to 500,000 CZK. Those are numbers a Czech owner signs in one meeting and a CEE cluster routes to a procurement panel for two quarters. 43 entities screened on this one question produced 8 workable accounts, and it is the only screen in the file that has ever changed a decision.

**Estate match, because we sell a room, not a market.** The product is a read of the shoppers standing in a specific instrumented store, matched to that store's receipts. Data from one chain in a category is a channel read, never a market average and never a benchmark. A brand that is not on that shelf is not a prospect at any size.

**Rights status, because capability is not permission.** Almost every mandate is unsigned today. What may be shown, to whom, and when is gated by the mandate, not by what the platform can compute. Selling ahead of the paper is the single most expensive mistake available in this pillar.

**The residual, not the who-question.** A brand already buying retailer loyalty data believes the who-question is answered, and at least one global CPG is on record crediting the retailer networks with answering it. What we sell is the residual: the unidentified, non-loyalty-card shopper standing in front of the fixture, whom a card-based product cannot see, and the gap between visitors and buyers. Never say "nobody knows this".

**The calendar is itself a qualification filter.** A brand not in conversation by the end of October is a 2027 conversation. Budget lock runs from late September to mid-November. December is unspent-budget season and needs a signed order plus a PO raised before year end.

**Capacity is the hard constraint.** 8 accounts at 50 founder-hours each, across 11 weeks, is 36 hours a week: the founder's entire calendar. Slot 9 does not exist. Every account added is an account removed.

## How outreach uses this page

1. **Registry first, before anything else.** Pull the ARES or ORSR extract and set `is_statutory_officer`. Do it before the first meeting, not after.
2. **Check estate match.** If the brand is not on a shelf we instrument, record no_estate and stop. Do not book the meeting.
3. **Run the margin test** from the Sbírka listin filing before any price is named. Above ~2% of net profit, the offer is a study, not a seat.
4. **Check rights status.** It decides whether this is an invoice conversation or a methodology-only conversation ending in a conditional order.
5. **Identify the persona,** and at an owner-managed firm create two Person records, not three: the pen and one commercial lead. See the Target Personas page.
6. **Log tier, autonomy and autonomy evidence in the CRM** when creating the Organization.
7. **Ask the autonomy question at the end of the methodology call, before any price is named:** "Would that be your signature, or would it need to clear above Prague?" A "yes, that's me" moves the account up a tier. A hesitation is worth more than another week of desk research.
8. **Tier 3 requires founder approval before a meeting is booked.**

## Open items that change this page

- **Founder decision, unresolved.** Is retailer-side cash (estate-holder, private label, distributor) bookable against the brand goal? Tier 1 above assumes yes. If the answer is no, Tier 1 loses GymBeam and Plaček and the brand pillar has no 2026 signature at all.
- **The Brand-Worn Index track** needs no retailer mandate and is the only unmandated 2026 revenue line. 16 of 18 candidate apparel companies are single- or dual-signature CZ/SK entities. It is gated on the 1 October fill-rate result, and the tiering above needs an apparel row once that lands.
- **Beaphar Eastern Europe s.r.o. must not be promoted.** Dušan Plaček is a serving jednatel of the entity we would invoice. Booking it as an independent third-party seat would misreport the goal.
