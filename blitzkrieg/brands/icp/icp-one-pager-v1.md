## 1. WHO

**Not a company, a buying centre of two people.** At the eight Bin-A targets the buying centre is
the **statutory officer** plus **one commercial lead**, and they are frequently the same human that
three separate researchers each claimed as their own persona. Collapse them before building the
outreach list, or 8 accounts x 3 contacts x 50 hours exceeds the founder's entire calendar.

| Rank | Who | Why them |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Owner / jednatel / statutory-officer GM** | The only signature that provably stays in the country. Decides in one meeting, no procurement, no legal department. |
| 2 | **Trade / shopper marketing manager** | Biggest discretionary in-store line, lowest provenance bar, buys the FIRST purchase (the study). |
| 3 | **Sales / commercial director** | Economic buyer only where he is himself a registered statutory officer. |
| 4 | **Private-label / own-brand lead** | Brand-like P&L, real unmet need, and uniquely **no incumbent supplier to displace**. |
| 5 | **Distributor / importer commercial lead** | One door controlling dozens of brands that have no CZ entity at all. |

**Champion, never buyer:** the KAM. **Blocker to neutralise first:** legal/DPO.
**Do not spend hours on:** local NPD, field/Perfect Store, CEE insights lead, retail media buyer,
e-commerce manager. All five researched and rejected.

## 2. BUDGET LINE

**Never say "research" and never say "innovation".** There is usually no research line in a CZ
subsidiary at all, and the Czech research market shrank 5.5% to CZK 3.96bn in 2025.

- **Trade / shopper marketing / podpora prodeje** is the target line. It is the largest
  discretionary spend in the building and the least well-evidenced.
- **Owner's P&L**, booked to marketing or a one-off project line. His accountant asks only whether
  it is *náklad* or *investice*.
- **A test bucket, not a source-of-record subscription.** This is also what keeps the decision with
  the Czech signature instead of routing it to a regional research procurement panel.
- **It is zero-sum, not new money.** IAB Europe's Marie-Clare Puffett: *"marketers say they're
  moving money from other channels into retail media and they're not creating new budgets. There's
  not new money"*, with linear TV and performance marketing named as the sources, and separate
  practitioner survey data putting shopper and trade budgets first in line. We have no direct
  evidence that a *measurement* purchase is funded the same way, but assume it is and pre-empt
  *"co to nahrazuje?"* rather than being surprised by it.
- Ladder: study **150 to 500k CZK** (not a seat) then founding seat **EUR 40k floor**.
  EUR 40k = CZK ~980,000. Run the margin test against filed net profit before naming a price.

## 3. TRIGGER EVENT

The calendar is the qualification filter. **A brand not in conversation by end-October is a 2027
conversation.**

| When | Trigger | Who it fires for |
|---|---|---|
| Aug to Oct | Next year's conditions strategy and trade-investment envelope set | Sales director |
| Late Aug to Oct | The *ceník* goes to the chains | KAM |
| Sep to Nov | Brief-writing season for next year's campaigns | Brand manager |
| **Late Sep to mid-Nov** | **Budget lock. The hardest date in the pillar.** | All commercial roles |
| Nov to Dec | The *roční jednání* with the chains; new conditions signed for 1 January | KAM, trade |
| **December** | **Unspent current-year budget must be committed. A signed order plus a PO raised before year-end.** | Sales director, procurement, finance |
| Jan | SOCR CR + NielsenIQ publish the annual retail analysis; Forbes publishes the family-firm ranking; Samoska convenes | Owner, sales director |
| Any time | A lost or won listing, a chain opening a category review, a principal threatening to go direct, a chain launching retail-media inventory | Trade, KAM, distributor |

Discovery question that finds it: **"co se změnilo?"** / *what changed recently?*

## 4. FIRST OBJECTION

What they actually say in minute three, and what is behind it.

| Persona | The objection | What is really behind it |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | *"Já jsem v té prodejně každý týden. Svého zákazníka znám."* | Status and identity, not scepticism. **Never contradict head-on.** |
| **Owner** | *"Kolik to stojí a co za to dostanu příští čtvrtletí?"* | *Náklad* or *investice*, against what return. He has no research reflex. |
| **Sales director** | *"Co to nahrazuje? Nemám na to řádek."* | The budget-bucket problem: the number-one recorded obstacle from the only primary conversation on file. |
| **Sales director** | *"Jedna prodejna. To přece nemůžu vydávat za trh."* | He is right, he knows it, and he is testing whether we will admit it. **Admitting it fast is the single fastest credibility move available.** |
| **Trade** | *"A o kolik nám to zvedlo prodej?"* | He is bonused on sell-out, not on shopper understanding. |
| **Trade** | *"Data z prodejen mi dává řetězec."* | Not a claim the incumbent is good: a signal that in-store money is already contractually committed. |
| **KAM** | *"Můžete mi dát můj podíl proti konkurentovi v tomhle řetězci?"* | **The toxic cut. Refuse it in minute three: refusing is what makes everything else believable.** |
| **KAM** | *"Kvartálně a se zpožděním? Moje jednání je příští úterý."* | He assumes value equals freshness. Answer with the buyer-approved reframe (below). |
| **Brand mgr** | *"Persony máme. Máme brand tracking, máme panel."* | Status: personas are what his own function produces. Lead with measurement, never personas. |
| **Private label** | *"Vy tohle chcete prodávat i našim dodavatelům."* | The disintermediation fear. One of only two genuinely real retailer fears. |
| **Legal** | *"Tohle je biometrická kategorizace podle AI Actu."* | Article 50 live since 2 Aug 2026, Czech adaptation act still unadopted, zero upside for them. |
| **Procurement** | *"Máme preferované dodavatele na market research."* | Routing to a regional panel, which is the difference between a 2026 and a 2027 deal. |
| **Any data-literate buyer** | *"Demografie nic neřekne."* Prince Charles and Prince William are the same demographic and shop nothing alike; basket data beats it. | The primary objection to a demographics-led pitch, from Wolt's Global Head of CPG Partnerships. Position inferred demographics as an in-aisle **denominator** (who had the chance to buy), never as a segmentation competing with basket data. |
| **Any senior buyer** | *"Nám nechybí data."* / We do not lack data, we lack the ability to act on it. | Said by a podcast host speaking in brand persona (colour, not a datapoint), but echoed by a real buyer: Henkel's Global Head of eCommerce named *"we kept adding data sources but we didn't take care of the governance enough"* as his biggest failure. Our category's default pitch lands directly on that scar. **Never open with "here is a new signal".** |
| **Any brand already buying retailer data** | *"Kdo je náš zákazník, nám řekne řetězec."* / The chain's own data already tells us who our shopper is. | Not hypothetical. Kimberly-Clark credits dunnhumby and Nectar 360 with helping it *"actually really truly understand our shoppers"*, and no buyer in any of the three passes complains that the who-question is unanswered. **Answer with the residual, never with "nobody knows this":** the unidentified, non-loyalty-card shopper in front of the fixture, whom a card-based product cannot see. |
| **Retailer / retail-media lead** | *"Měřit se toho dá spousta. Znamená to, že to měřit musíme?"* | REWE's Head of Retail Media Connect, near verbatim. He is short of hours and political capital, not of ideas: Iceland's equivalent says 80% of the job is keeping internal peace. Sell him internal legitimacy, not another project. |
| **Any media budget owner** | Usually unsaid: *"a co když to dopadne špatně?"* | Correction (n) in `icp-brands.md`. Honest measurement grades his last two years and typically looks worse than what was sold to him. Silent non-adoption, not an objection. **Route around him to whoever gains from a true number.** |

**The one line that answers the biggest objection:**
> *"This is not ammunition your buyer will resent. It is evidence your buyer has already accepted."*

The retailer holds 90-day insight priority and a veto, so anything a brand receives has already been
seen and approved by its buyer. The publication lag is the credential, not the concession.

## 5. BUYING PROCESS

1. **Qualify on autonomy before category.** Can this entity sign ~EUR 40k without the signature
   leaving the country? 43 entities screened, 8 survive. Ask it at the *end* of the methodology
   call, before any price is named.
2. **Neutralise legal first**, with a document rather than a meeting. **Sequencing caveat from the
   corpus pass (icp-brands §2h):** legal-first is right for a retailer, a DPO or a regulated
   category, where privacy is likely the first question raised. At a Bin-A owner with no legal
   function it manufactures a doubt he did not have: hold the pack and deploy it on request. Six pages: AI Act role map
   (the retailer is the deployer carrying the Article 50(3) duty), GDPR basis, DPIA, the k>=25
   architecture, and the volunteered list of cuts we will contractually never build. Get past legal
   and marketing will engage; the same pack also unblocks procurement and the analyst.
   **Do not lean on GDPR adequacy alone:** the two in-store vendors nearest our position already
   market GDPR compliance as a feature, so it reads as parity. The k>=25 gate and the
   no-identification design are the actual argument.
3. **Sell the study, not the seat.** 150 to 500k CZK from the activation or test bucket. Frame as
   a test, never as a source of record. **Shape it as a bounded proof:** eight to twelve weeks, one
   hard before-and-after number, one decision. That is the shape of every operator AI win the
   corpus records, and buyers describe themselves as stuck in pilot purgatory, so a narrow proof
   beats a broad promise.
4. **Send the method annex BEFORE the meeting**, not after. Where a commercial analyst exists, his
   verification decides whether our number is allowed into any internal deck at all.
5. **Pre-empt procurement** with a complete onboarding file nobody asked for, and propose the spend
   classification yourself (trade services, not market research).
6. **Close two budget years in one sitting:** a study order against this year and a subscription
   order for next, with the density escrow as the refund condition.

**Bin-A note:** steps 2, 4 and 5 mostly collapse. There is no procurement, no legal department and
no analyst. **Do not deploy "we can make you safe" framing on an owner: it manufactures a doubt he
did not have and reads as weakness.** He carries money risk, not career risk.

## 6. CONSUMPTION FORMAT

**PRIMARY (P&G, banked):** *quarterly Excel is enough; real-time is not needed; chat is wanted for
insight.* Do not over-build. Deliver in the shape they already consume and put the chat on top.

| Persona | What they will actually open |
|---|---|
| Owner | **One A4 decision page.** Finding, two or three moves, method annex behind it. |
| Sales director | **One slide. Literally one.** Or a PDF whose page one is the decision layer. |
| Trade | **One slide with the scope statement in its own footer**, because it will be forwarded without us. Plus Excel shaped like the incumbent report. |
| KAM | One slide into his PowerPoint deck, plus a two-page method annex. |
| Brand manager | One slide stating the contradiction in a single sentence; a short PDF of persona cards. |
| Distributor | Excel shaped like the item report he already circulates, plus a one-paragraph "so what". |
| Analyst | The method annex as a PDF, plus the underlying counts in Excel, **sent before the meeting**. |
| Legal | One bound PDF, forwardable unedited to a group privacy function. |

**Every output must be presentable to the retailer's buyer as CATEGORY evidence, not only brand
evidence.** Ex-CCO Europe at Coca-Cola: *"you'll probably not be interested in selling more of brand
A if this is a zero sum game that results in selling less of brand B."* The corpus's clearest
transferable mechanism, and most CPGs still pitch brand-centrically, so it is a differentiator.

**Every chart carries its own scope footer**: estate, store count, period, n, k>=25 gate, age as an
estimate with error bands, "this store, not the chain". A slide without it cannot be forwarded
safely, and it will be forwarded.

**Corroborated by the corpus, with one addition.** Both the incumbent and the buyer independently
say a **data-first pitch fails and a use-case-first pitch wins** (NIQ's SVP: *"inverse completely
the pyramid... what are the 10, 20, 30 use cases"*; Henkel: *"not to start with the data, start with
the application"*). So the first three slides must be three decisions the client makes differently,
with the observation layer revealed afterwards as the minimum data required.
**And drop the latency story:** no voice in 232 episodes asks for real-time insight data, and
Kimberly-Clark defends a six-to-twelve-month panel cadence as sufficient. Real-time is wanted only
for operational and supply-chain data. Speed is not a selling point to this buyer.
**Match the register as well as the structure:** across this corpus, operators' own modest,
caveat-laden numbers read as credible and vendors' big narrative claims read as less so. Understate,
and put the limits on the same page as the finding.

**Not available:** a chat layer over the aggregates (the thing P&G asked for by name) is not
deliverable today, while NIQ Ask Arthur, Kantar KaiA and Circana Complete Why all ship one.
