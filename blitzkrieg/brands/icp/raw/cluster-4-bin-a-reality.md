## ROLE-EXISTENCE VERDICT

FOUR DIFFERENT VERDICTS, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME SHAPE.

(1) OWNER / JEDNATEL / STATUTORY-OFFICER COUNTRY GM — EXISTS LOCALLY, WITH BUDGET, AND IS THE ONLY PERSONA IN THE WHOLE BRAND SET WHOSE €40k SIGNATURE PROVABLY STAYS INSIDE THE BORDER. This is not a hypothesis: registry evidence in `targets/DECISION.md` §5A puts a Czech- or Slovak-resident owner-signer at GymBeam (Cicman 72%), Plaček (Dušan Plaček or Rejchrt, each alone), CBI/DanCzek (Poul, 65% owner AND chairman, board tenures 22–30 years, all resident within ~10 km), VAFO (every officer a Bouška), Kofola (its own ultimate parent), Mattoni, Nutrend (single monistic board member), Big Shock (three jednatelé, each alone), Dermacol, Teta/Solvent, JIP (three-generation family board), PEAL, GECO (chairman is one of four owners), and — the only multinational shape that qualifies — BAT ČR, where Štěpán Michlíček has been GM and a registered jednatel since 2014 and SK runs as an organizačná zložka so one signature covers both countries. In these firms five corporate roles collapse into one or two people. Of 43 unique legal entities screened, 8 survive as workable €40k-capable local pens.

(2) LOCAL NPD / INNOVATION / PRODUCT AND PACK — LARGELY A MIRAGE AS A SEPARATE FUNCTION, AND ENTIRELY A MIRAGE IN MULTINATIONAL SUBSIDIARIES. Empirically: jobs.cz for "innovation manager" returns 287 hits of which effectively none is FMCG — the title in Czechia belongs to IT, banking and consulting (KPMG, EY, Raiffeisenbank, SOFTEC). What actually exists in Czech FMCG is a *technolog* / *pracovník vývoje* / *specialista vývoje potravin* at 40,000–55,000 CZK, sitting in a factory (Kofola's "Pracovník vývoje – Kofola Krnov" and four *technolog v potravinářské výrobě* posts; JIP's "Technolog výroby – cukrárny a lahůdek"; Bidfood, La Lorraine, Vitalogy), plus a *product manager* hybrid at 45,000–65,000 CZK (BRAINMARKET "PRODUCT MANAGER – FOOD (FMCG)"). profesia.sk repeats the pattern for Slovakia: "vývoj nových produktov" returns 44 roles, almost all automotive/industrial engineering, and exactly one FMCG food technologist at €1,300–1,500/month. None of these people holds a research budget or buys a data source. Where local NPD *decision* authority genuinely exists it sits with the owner himself (Bouška launching dog-microbiome analysis and buying Kennelpakaste; Zedník putting ~CZK 20m/year into development with external advisors and pro athletes; Cicman moving from proteins into longevity) or with a brand manager on a *locally owned brand* (Kofola's "Brand Manager pro značku Rajec" in Ostrava-Poruba and "Senior Brand Manager pro značku Holba"; Mondelez's "Brand Manager Opavia CZ & SK"). At a multinational subsidiary the innovation pipeline is global/regional and the country office executes a launch — treat any "CZ innovation manager" as a launch executor, not a buyer. PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE: do not build an NPD outreach track. Fold the NPD use cases into the owner's conversation (he is the NPD decision-maker) and into the private-label lead's conversation (the retailer's category manager JD literally contains "řízení uvedení nových produktů na český a slovenský trh a vyhodnocení jejich komerčních výsledků"). This saves the founder the ~50 hours a fourth outreach track would cost.

(3) DISTRIBUTOR / IMPORTER COMMERCIAL LEAD — EXISTS LOCALLY, WITH BUDGET, AND IS STRUCTURALLY THE HIGHEST-LEVERAGE DOOR IN THE FILE, BECAUSE ONE DOOR CONTROLS DOZENS OF BRANDS. Plaček's wholesale arm publishes its own brand book carrying ~155 supplier brands and distributes to 1,000+ customers (pet shops, vets, e-shops); Trixie, Tetra, Eukanuba/IAMS, Hagen, Versele-Laga and dozens more have NO Czech brand entity holding a budget at all. PEAL supplies EuroOil Loket 2–3× weekly across a 5,000+ item book; JIP is 22bn CZK of revenue and ~900m CZK EBITDA with a three-generation family board; GECO is the largest tobacco distributor with a chairman who is one of four owners. In every one of these the pen and the commercial lead are Czech-resident. Two structural warnings: the commercial lead at a large wholesaler may be a *junior category buyer* with no budget (the file already flags this for JIP, where tobacco is a minority of the business), and selling a distributor a demographic read on brands it distributes creates an asymmetry the brand owners did not consent to.

(4) RETAILER PRIVATE-LABEL / OWN-BRAND TEAM BUYING LIKE A BRAND — EXISTS LOCALLY, WITH BUDGET, AND IS THE ONE SANCTIONED EXCEPTION TO "BRANDS ONLY". The role is visible in Czech job ads as a real, senior, P&L-owning job: BENU's "BUSINESS UNIT MANAGER – Private Label & Own Brands" ("řídit privátní značku a její kategorie jako samostatnou jednotku", explicit P&L accountability) and "BRAND MANAGER privátních značek"; Teta/Solvent's "Category manager pro vybrané kategorie" at 90,000–110,000 CZK; VAFO PRIVATE LABEL hiring its own demand planner. At Plaček it is not a separate advertised role — the file's own inference is that "realistically the private-label / category director signs and Rejchrt approves". This persona has brand-like margins, brand-like need for shopper insight, and no incumbent read on its own shoppers — but it is also, at Plaček and Teta, the mandate partner, so the revenue must be scoped and priced deliberately or it will be absorbed into the mandate negotiation and disappear.

### Day in the life
Monday 07:00 he opens the daily sales sheet himself — obrat, marže, obrátka, per store or per line — in Excel or a simple BI export, not a dashboard someone built for him. Weekly obchodní porada with the commercial director and the marketing manager, in which he personally approves the promo prices, the new SKU, the pack artwork and the trade-fair budget. He walks a store or the production line unannounced at least weekly and reads the shelf with his own eyes; he still recognises regular customers. Mid-week: one chain buyer or one big customer on the phone about zadní marže, a listing or a bonus. He signs invoices above a threshold and every contract. Thursday: bank, auditor, or a family-board conversation about the next factory / the next country. Quarterly he gives an interview to Forbes, E15, Hospodářské noviny, Ekonom or Seznam Zprávy Byznys, and in January his revenue lands in the Forbes Největší rodinné firmy ranking next to a rival's. His conference calendar is thin and status-driven: Samoška in Olomouc in late January, Retail Summit in Prague in early March, Czech Retail Days on 5–6 November, POPAI DAY at O2 universum on 19 November. Annual accounts (účetní závěrka) and the Sbírka listin filing are a real date in his year. He answers his own email.

### Pains
- [critical] He has no measurement of who stands in front of his product and does not buy it. He has sell-out (his own or the chain's), he has a loyalty file if he is a retailer, and both only describe people who already bought :: Every current CZ data source in his world is transactional; the visitor-vs-buyer gap is exactly what the M&M conversion funnel measures (dashboard `conversion-funnel`, attention thresholds ≥1.5s/≥2.5s), and nothing he buys today produces it.
- [critical] Asymmetry at the chain negotiation: he arrives with numbers the chain gave him :: `14-retailer-data-objection.md` and the JBP literature both describe the negotiation as running on the retailer's data; "zadní marže" is a standing CZ trade-press war (ProfitOnline); AČTO's Březina says small operators "nejsou schopny levně nakoupit od dodavatelů".
- [high] Syndicated data is priced and shaped for multinationals, so his substitute is free trade-press headlines :: NielsenIQ presents "Co se vyplatí mít v regálech" free at Samoška and co-publishes with SOCR ČR; the CZ research market shrank 5.5% to CZK 3.96bn in 2025 with projects moving "do správy zahraničních entit" (SIMAR via MediaGuru). He is not an NIQ client and knows the price would not survive his own margin test.
- [high] Private-label and discounter pressure on his tier :: YouGov shopper panel via Zboží a Prodej — private labels appeared in 61% of all CZ purchases in 2025; in discounters they are in nearly 90% of baskets.
- [medium] He cannot tell whether a launch failed on the product or on the placement :: he has no shelf-level observation, only despatch and sell-out; the technolog who made it and the KAM who sold it blame each other.
- [medium] Succession and the second generation :: the CZ family-firm cohort founded post-1989 is in its first handover wave; only ~a third cross it (VŠE Centre for Family Businesses via the Podniko podcast).
- [low] Regulatory noise he must answer with evidence he does not have :: the under-16 energy-drink bill was still at working-group stage as of 29 Jun 2026 with the age limit raised from 15 to 16 — a debate about who actually buys, conducted without shelf-level evidence.
- [WE DO NOT SOLVE THIS] He wants volume, a listing and a better annual deal. We cannot move any of them. We have no cross-visit identity so we can never give him repeat rate, frequency, loyalty or "did the campaign bring the same person back"; we never touch the retailer's margin or elasticity; and our brand-facing reads carry a deliberate publication lag precisely so they cannot be used as ammunition inside a live negotiation cycle. Say this before he discovers it.

### How to reach them
Direct, from the founder's own address, Tuesday–Thursday around 07:45, ≤120 words, one question, no attachment, no link, no deck. Never "rádi bychom vám představili naše řešení" — that is a vendor email and it dies. The opening line must be about HIS shelf and must contradict something he believes, e.g.: "Dobrý den, pane Zedníku — v kamenné prodejně sportovní výživy jsme 21 dní měřili, kdo stojí u regálu a kdo z něj skutečně bere; 92 % nákupů se nám podařilo spárovat s účtenkou. Jedno číslo o tom, kdo kupuje prémiovou řadu, mi přijde, že se neshoduje s tím, na koho míří kampaně. Máte 20 minut, abych vám ukázal metodu a zeptal se na váš názor?" Ask for 20 minutes, named duration. Better than any email: a warm path through another owner in the Forbes family-firms list, through the retailer who convenes the room (ČEPRO did exactly this for JTI), or a handshake at Samoška / Czech Retail Days / POPAI DAY. Do not quote a price in the first meeting; the answer to "kolik to stojí" is "to záleží na tom, jaké pokrytí byste potřeboval". Ask the autonomy question at the END, conversationally, before any price.

### Kill risks
- He buys the CZK 300k study and never the €40k seat — 'complementary' is a price cap and an owner is exactly the buyer who will take the cheap half. The two-document close (2026 study order + 2027 subscription order, one sitting) exists because of this risk.
- The margin test kills him: Dallmayr at 86% of net profit and AG Foods at 25% are not seats at any effort level, and several apparel names have no current published revenue figure at all, so the price cannot be set honestly.

### Objections

- **""Já jsem v té prodejně každý týden. Svého zákazníka znám.""**
  - *Really about:* Status and identity, not scepticism about data. His authority in his own firm rests on being the one who understands the business from the floor up. A vendor who implies otherwise is attacking the thing he is proudest of. He is also, in fact, partly right — he knows his buyers well. What he does not know, and cannot know from standing in the store, is who came, looked and left, or what the composition of the aisle looks like across 7,000 visitors and 21 days.
  - *Best answer:* "Určitě znáte. Vy vidíte lidi, kteří u vás nakoupili — a ty znáte líp než kdokoliv jiný. My měříme ty, co si to vzali do ruky a vrátili to zpátky. Za 21 dní v jedné prodejně: 7 475 návštěvníků, 2 622 nákupů spárovaných s účtenkou, 92 %. Ten rozdíl mezi těmi dvěma čísly je jediné, co prodáváme. A jestli vám z toho vypadne něco, co už víte, řeknu vám to rovnou a nebudeme si nic účtovat." Then show one finding that contradicts something he believes — the teaser must be counterintuitive, not impressive.
- **""Kolik to stojí a co za to dostanu příští čtvrtletí? My nekupujeme data, my kupujeme růst.""**
  - *Really about:* He is applying his real decision rule: is this náklad or investice, and against what return. He has no research budget line to route it to, so it competes directly with a machine, a store or a van. He is also testing whether we will inflate the promise.
  - *Best answer:* Do not quote a price in meeting one; the honest answer is "to záleží na tom, jaké pokrytí byste potřeboval." Then be concrete about the return without inventing one: "Nekupujete růst, kupujete jedno rozhodnutí, které dneska děláte poslepu — jaká gramáž, jaký facing, na koho míří kampaň. Začínáme studií za 150 až 500 tisíc, ne předplatným. Když z ní nevypadne nic, co by změnilo jedno rozhodnutí, nemá smysl pokračovat." Then close with the two-document mechanic — a 2026 study order and a 2027 subscription order signed in one sitting — because letting him split it is how the seat becomes a study.
- **""To je ta věc s kamerami? Jak je to s GDPR — a nechci mít problém s Úřadem.""**
  - *Really about:* Money and reputation risk, not career risk. He has no DPO and no legal department; he will send it to an external law firm, which adds two to four weeks. He is not looking for reassurance, he is looking for a document he can forward.
  - *Best answer:* Answer in one paragraph and hand over paper: "Žádné rozpoznávání obličejů, žádné identity, žádné biometrické šablony, žádné sledování mezi návštěvami. Věk je odhad s intervalem spolehlivosti, nikdy ne detekce a nikdy ne ověření. Ven jde jen agregát od 25 lidí výš. Zpracování probíhá u vás, my dostáváme popis, ne fotku." Then give him the methodology dossier and the DPIA pack so his lawyer reviews a document rather than writing one. Do NOT lead with this and do not over-reassure — with an owner, a compliance-led opening manufactures a doubt he did not have.
- **""Ale moje objemy jsou v Albertu, Kauflandu a v Lidlu — tam vy neměříte.""**
  - *Really about:* The single most legitimate objection in the whole file, and it is fatal wherever it is true. A forecourt or pet-specialist read is 1–3% of a beverage brand's channel revenue and is indefensible as an account read at €40k.
  - *Best answer:* Concede it immediately and reprice, or walk. "Máte pravdu a nebudu vám tvrdit něco jiného. Tohle není čtení vašeho trhu, je to čtení jednoho kanálu — a jestli je pro vás ten kanál okrajový, je to studie za pár set tisíc, ne roční předplatné." If the channel genuinely does not matter to him, this account is not a seat and should be downgraded or dropped, not argued with. The strategy pack already killed the whole beverages track on exactly this arithmetic.

### Day in the life
The technolog's week: sensory panel on Tuesday, three sample batches, a supplier's new raw material to qualify, a shelf-life trial, specification and label copy for the regulator, a PPWR packaging question from the buyer, a complaint to close out. He works in grams, °C, days of shelf life, and cost per unit. The product/brand manager's week: a price ladder in Excel, artwork rounds with an external studio, a listing presentation for a chain buyer, sell-out from the chain's portal, a promo calendar. Neither of them commissions research. When the owner tastes the sample and says yes, it is launched; when the chain buyer says the price is wrong, it is reformulated. The single moment they touch consumer evidence is a tasting panel of colleagues, a handful of reviews, or whatever the trade press printed from NielsenIQ that month.

### Jobs to be done
- When I choose between two pack formats, I want to know which shopper type physically picks up which one at the shelf, so I stop guessing at the format decision.

- When I develop a premium tier, I want to know whether the premium shopper is in this channel at all, so I do not build a line for a shopper who shops elsewhere.
- When the owner asks me why we should launch this, I want one observed number rather than a taste opinion, so the decision is not purely aesthetic.

### Kill risks
- HE IS THE MANDATE PARTNER. Selling the retailer its own private-label insight while negotiating the measurement mandate collapses two negotiations into one, and the revenue is the thing that gets given away. This is a self-inflicted risk entirely inside the founder's control and it requires a written decision memo before the meeting.
- Booking him as a paying 'brand seat' misreports the goal if the goal is third-party brands. He is a principled exception to brands-only, not a substitute for one — and at Beaphar, where Dušan Plaček is a serving jednatel of the entity we would invoice, he cannot be counted as an independent third-party seat at all.
- Firewall design, not just contract language: if Plaček's own-brand team and VAFO both hold seats, the aggregates each sees must be constructed so neither can read the other's demographics out of them. VAFO also manufactures private label and reportedly makes Sam's Field for a rival wholesaler, so a Super zoo read hands a private-label manufacturer a view of its largest retail customer's own-brand programme. This is a product and aggregation constraint, not only a sales one.
- Everything measurable today is one store over 28 days in peak tick season. It is a store read, never a chain read, and he will want the chain.
- His single most wanted metric — do own-brand buyers come back — is structurally impossible for us and better answered by his own loyalty card.
- At Teta a substantial franchised store share may mean the retail entity cannot unilaterally commit locations to camera installation; that is a mandate-scope question that should be raised early rather than discovered late.
- Sportisimo's private-label cluster looks like this persona and is not: Czech-owned, but the decision sits in central merchandising rather than in a discrete brand-marketing budget.

### Objections

- **""Mám věrnostní kartu. O svém zákazníkovi vím všechno.""**
  - *Really about:* Genuine pride in an asset that really is good, plus the industry line he hears constantly — that retailers have the best access to consumer preference and can translate it straight into a private-label brief. He is defending the premise of his own job.
  - *Best answer:* "Věrnostní karta je nejlepší věc, kterou máte, a my ji nenahrazujeme. Vidí ale jenom členy a jenom nákupy. Neuvidí člověka, který nemá kartu, a hlavně neuvidí toho, kdo se zastavil u vašeho facingu, porovnal Dog Fantasy s Trixie a odešel s tou druhou. To je přesně ten člověk, kvůli kterému neplníte cíl podílu vlastních značek — a je neviditelný ve všem, co dneska máte. My ho umíme popsat, agregovaně, od 25 lidí výš. Opakovaný nákup si nechte u sebe, ten umíte líp než my."
- **""Vy tohle chcete prodávat i našim dodavatelům. Proč bych vám měl pouštět naše prodejny?""**
  - *Really about:* The disintermediation fear, which the file identifies as one of only two genuinely real retailer fears, plus the specific worry that a private-label manufacturer who is also a shelf competitor (VAFO, which manufactures private label and is 29.7% of third-party revenue on this shelf) learns about his own-brand programme.
  - *Best answer:* "Rovnou tři věci, které jsou v mandátu, ne v marketingu. Za prvé, obchodní data — marže, sell-through, elasticita — nikdy nedostaneme a nedostanou je ani značky, protože je vůbec nesbíráme. Za druhé, žádný řez značka versus jmenovaný konkurent u jednoho řetězce a nikdy žádný rozpad vaší privátní značky ve výstupu pro dodavatele. Za třetí, máte veto na každý výstup, devadesátidenní přednost před syndikací, a brand-facing čtení jde ven se zpožděním. A rovnou přiznám asymetrii: pokud by seat držela VAFO i váš own-brand tým, agregáty musí být postavené tak, aby si ani jeden nemohl vyčíst demografii toho druhého. To je konstrukční podmínka, ne slib."
- **""Naše vlastní značka je cenová hra. Nakupující se rozhoduje podle ceny, ne podle toho, kdo je.""**
  - *Really about:* A defensible position that is also his industry's old story, and one his own trade press has already abandoned — private labels are now described as a full part of the assortment setting the market's direction, and as a platform for local production and innovation. He half wants to be argued out of it, because a price play has no career in it.
  - *Best answer:* "Kdyby to byla jenom cena, tak by u vás nefungovaly prémiové řady — a u vás fungují: Ontario je 14,1 % všech tržeb v té prodejně. Cena rozhoduje o tom, jestli si toho člověk všimne. O tom, jestli sáhne, rozhoduje pořád ještě obal, police a to, kdo u ní stojí. Přesně to umíme změřit: kdo se zastavil, jak dlouho, u které cenové hladiny, a co si nakonec odnesl — všechno agregovaně, jedna prodejna, konkrétní okno, u každého čísla počet košíků."
