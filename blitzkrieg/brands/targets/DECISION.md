Of the rest: BAT is the cleanest pen (12-year jednatel, one signature for CZ+SK), PMI the biggest wallet and slowest legal, Imperial the fastest org with the thinnest presence. **Pet food & pet care — REAL for one (Plaček); everything else CONDITIONAL on a mandate that has no greenlight.**
The feared blocker was wrong and the aim was wrong. Own-brand density does not make Super zoo unsellable, but the SKU count overstated how open the estate is: measured on **28 consecutive days of receipts** (store CS314, 7 Jul – 4 Aug 2026; 13,153 lines, 2.06m CZK gross) the split of brandable revenue is **own 45.2% / third-party 47.2%**, ≈48.9 / 51.1 with the 7.6% unattributed residual allocated pro-rata. The giants fail here for a **listing** reason, not a channel-coverage one, and the distinction is the whole meeting. Mars and Nestlé are **not** absent from the premium specialist channel — they lead it: Royal Canin holds ~30% of European high-end and distributes specifically through pet specialists and vets, and Pro Plan is sold specialist-exclusive. Receipt-level proof, stronger than the earlier SKU inference (2.1% / 3.8%), which is retained only as evidence of assortment *breadth*.

**Two sentences are therefore banned in any Purina or Mars conversation, and both would be unrecoverable:** never tell them they are "absent from the premium specialist channel" (false, and their category team knows their own numbers), and never promise competitor-level cuts from a single chain — at n=1 "the channel" is a synonym for Super zoo, which makes it the prohibited named-retailer × named-brand × named-competitor cut, with Plaček's own private label as the competitor.
