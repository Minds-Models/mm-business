The VC Case: De-risking the Business Model

Why we are exploring this and how to frame it to investors:

  • The Investor Objection: VCs will push back on our model because installing physical cameras requires Capex, heavy retailer legal/IT friction, and is slow to scale.
  • The Asset-Light Pivot: If we can piggyback on existing infrastructure (cameras already inside stores), our business becomes a pure Software/API play. This drastically increases scaling speed and reduces Capex.
  • The Strategy: We need to prove we are actively exploring partnerships with entities that already have "eyes" in the store or have alternative ways to identify the "Who".
Target Alternative Data Profiles

The 4 avenues we are exploring to bypass our own hardware:

  • Existing Retailer CCTV (TOP PRIORITY): Can we deploy an edge-processing appliance to tap into the retailer's existing security camera feeds instead of installing our own sensors? (Requires retailer buy-in, but removes hardware installation). This is closest to our current use-case and must be pursued most aggressively.
  • DOOH (Digital Out of Home) Networks (Exploratory): Companies like POS Media, Lurity, etc., operate digital billboards inside retail stores. They often already have embedded cameras and negotiated privacy rights. Can we license their visual feed?
  • Smart POS & Self-Checkouts (Exploratory): Vendors (like NCR, Dotykačka) whose hardware faces the consumer and already knows exactly what was purchased. Do they have camera capabilities or loyalty linkages we can tap?
  • Telco / Mobility Data Fallback (Exploratory): O2, T-Mobile, Vodafone. Can we fuse their macro footfall/demographic data with our POS data to approximate the "Who", abandoning visual data entirely if GDPR becomes too strict?
The Re-engagement Pitch: Turning POS Providers into Partners

How to frame the deal to POS providers (like PVA Systems) to bypass data-theft objections and secure a fast pilot:

  • The Reframe (From Tech Cost to Revenue Generator): "We previously discussed using your POS data for personalization. We have pivoted. The real value is in Retail Media. We want to help your retail clients monetize their in-store audience, creating a brand new profit center for them."
  • The Win-Win-Win: PVA becomes the hero who brings a new revenue stream to their clients. Retailers monetize their data without needing data science teams. We provide the visual intelligence layer.
  • The "Zero-IT" Pilot Pitch: "We already have brand budgets (JTI/PMI) ready to deploy. We don't want direct access to your core system. Let's pick ONE innovative mid-size client of yours. We install our cameras. You simply provide a daily CSV/JSON export of receipts (timestamp + items). We handle all the analytics and pay for the pilot."
  • The Defense (Data Privacy): "We do NOT want you to share your client's data without their consent. We want to go to the client *together*. If the client agrees to the revenue opportunity, they will authorize the data export."
