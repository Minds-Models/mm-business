The Thesis (validated demand, named blocker, structural unlock)

Why this pillar exists — founder market intel

Bluetouch media and NANOVO were already asked by their customers for audience measurement — and the deal died on price-per-measurement, every time. Validated demand, named blocker.

Our unlock is structural, not incremental: dual monetization. One capture serves (a) the operator's measurement product AND (b) our second markets — Brand-Worn Index fill, audience norms, location-tied segments for programmatic, eval-sets. Because the data has a second life, we can price measurement at or below the wall that killed the predecessors. RMN estates are simultaneously a customer channel and a visual-only data source.

Reframe #1 — the “holy grail” is our moat

1:1 audience↔purchase is legally impossible in the EU — for everyone. We own the legal maximum: aggregated closed-loop lift at k≥25. Sales line: “the highest measurement resolution EU law permits.”

Reframe #2 — the price is half-known

Corridor from the research annex: audience-data fees 40–45% of CPM capped ~$3.50–4.00 · sales-lift measurement $0.75 CPM · ~$11/venue place-based onboarding · $9–49/sensor SaaS. The task in the live talks is validating the CZ corridor, not discovering prices from zero.

Reframe #3 — two threads, two deals

NANOVO media = operator: capture access + measurement, barter shape. Vistar = SSP: data distribution — location-tied segments buyers can act on. Never one conversation.

Hard requirement — the license or nothing

The operator contract must carry the k≥25 aggregate-resale license (+ Data Act clarity). No license → no second market → no dual-monetization discount → we hit the same price wall that killed everyone else. No measurement discount without the license signed.

Product Ladder (lowest friction first)

  1. Measurement API — counts / age / gender / dwell per screen; the operator reports however they like (white-label). The wedge.
  2. Per-creative attention — sellable NOW on the tobacco-funded wave as the P3 proof, no RMN partnership needed.
  3. Audience norms & location-tied segments — the SSP-facing product; segments tied to the DOOH location so buyers can react.
  4. Index feed — thousands of screens crush the index's 16-location requirement; RMN estates may be the index's best supply (fill gate Oct 1).

The Barter Shape

Mirror of the retailer trade: we give the operator the measurement capability their customers already demanded and they couldn't price; they give capture access + the aggregate license. Cash variants: paid API at ≥ marginal cost, or measurement-at-cost where second-market value justifies it — only with the license signed.

The Kill Criterion (written before the first call)

Kill on Nov 7 only if BOTH legs fail

(a) No second-market monetization path for non-POS visual data validates — index buyer interest, SSP segment appetite, and eval-set demand all cold — AND (b) no operator accepts barter or a paid API at ≥ our marginal cost.

Either leg alive → the pillar earns a 2027 line. A kill with a verdict is the system working; a pillar that limps past Nov 7 unruled is the failure mode.
