# Blitzkrieg — RMN pillar v0.2 (founder answers incorporated, 26 Aug)

**Scope:** discovery + two live threads. Budget ≤20 founder-hours. Decision memo **7 Nov**.

## 0. The thesis this pillar now has (from founder answer #11)

Operators (Bluetouch media, NANOVO) were ALREADY asked by their customers for audience
measurement — **the deal died on price-per-measurement every time.** That is a validated demand
with a named blocker. Our unlock is structural, not incremental: **dual monetization** — the same
visual capture serves (a) the operator's measurement product AND (b) our second markets (Brand-Worn
Index fill, audience norms, location-tied segments for programmatic, eval-sets). Because the data
has a second life, we can price measurement at or below the level that killed every prior attempt.
**RMN estates are simultaneously a customer channel and a data source (no POS — visual-only supply).**

## 1. Corrections & reframes (vs founder answers)

- **The 1:1 holy grail (answer #2) is legally impossible in the EU — for everyone.** Stop treating
  it as our gap; it is the ceiling of the market and we own the legal maximum: aggregated
  closed-loop lift (k≥25). Sell it as "the highest measurement resolution EU law permits."
- **"We don't know the price" (answer #1) is half-answered already.** Research-annex corridor:
  retailer audience-data fees 40–45% of CPM capped ~$3.50–4.00 · sales-lift measurement $0.75 CPM
  ($150k min spend, US anchor) · place-based measurement ~$11/venue onboarding (Geopath) · sensor
  SaaS $9–49/unit/mo. Task = validate the CZ corridor inside the two live threads, not research
  from zero.
- **Two live threads are two DIFFERENT deals — never one conversation:** **NANOVO media** =
  capture-access + measurement (operator deal, barter shape). **Vistar Media** = SSP/programmatic
  = data DISTRIBUTION (location-tied audience segments consumed by buyers). Run separately.
- **Rights discipline applies here exactly as with retailers:** the operator contract must carry
  the **k≥25 aggregate-resale license** (and Data Act clarity), or the "monetize elsewhere" leg —
  and with it the whole dual-monetization price hack — collapses. No license, no discount.

## 2. Product ladder (lowest friction first — founder answer #8)

1. **Measurement API** — counts/age/gender/dwell per screen; they report however they like
   (white-label). The wedge.
2. **Per-creative attention** (answer #6) — sellable NOW on the tobacco-funded wave as the P3
   proof, no RMN partnership needed.
3. **Audience norms & location-tied segments** — the SSP-facing product (answer #12: segments must
   be tied to the DOOH location so buyers can act).
4. **Index feed** — thousands of screens crush the index's 16-location requirement; RMN estates
   may be the index's best supply. (Cross-pillar: fill gate 1 Oct.)

## 3. The barter shape (answer #4)

Mirror of the retailer trade: **we give the operator a measurement capability their customers
already demanded and they couldn't price; they give capture access + the aggregate license.**
Cash variants: paid API at ≥ marginal cost; or measurement-at-cost where the second-market value
justifies it (only with the license signed).

## 4. Backbone (≤20 founder-hours total)

- **W1–2:** price-corridor memo (annex anchors + our cost floor at 10×); NANOVO media & Vistar
  threads mapped (who, stage, what they asked for); Bluetouch demand story documented as evidence;
  operator one-pager (API + barter option, license clause visible).

- **W7–9:** cheapest real test — ONE campaign measured at cost for one brand on existing screens;

- **7 Nov — decision memo:** 2027 line item or kill.
- **W11–13 (only if alive):** one operator term-sheet shape; index-feed assessment.

## 5. Kill criterion (founders' own, formalized — written before the first call)

Kill on 7 Nov **only if BOTH fail**: (a) no second-market monetization path for non-POS visual
data validates (index buyer interest, SSP segment appetite, eval-set demand all cold) **and**
(b) no operator accepts barter or a paid API at ≥ our marginal cost. Either leg alive → the pillar
earns a 2027 line. Acquirer-story question (answer #10, "could be") stays a watch item in the memo:
measurement-currency framing must lead; adtech framing never does.
