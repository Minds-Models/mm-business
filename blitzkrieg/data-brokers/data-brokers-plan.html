The Strategic Acquirer Pitch

How to frame the dialogue with Data Brokers (NIQ, Circana):

  • The Context: Data brokers measure what sold via POS data, but they lack the who (demographics, in-store behavior).
  • The Threat/Opportunity: We are building the "who" layer across multiple retailers with perpetual aggregate rights. If they don't buy us, someone else will, or we will disrupt their measurement monopoly.
  • The Approach: We are not selling the company now. We are initiating an "informational" relationship. Strategic premiums (8-12x multiples) go to companies the buyer has watched grow and become a threat over time.

Weekly SMART Goals

Week 1: Mapping the Acquirer Landscape Aug 24 - Aug 30
  • Research

    Identify Strategic Targets: Map out the key strategic acquirers in the consumer insights and measurement space (NielsenIQ, Circana, Kantar, dunnhumby, Nectar360). Identify the key M&A or Innovation leads within these organizations. Goal: Target List of 10-15 Contacts

Week 2: Defining the Informational Pitch Aug 31 - Sep 6
  • Strategy

    Draft Informational Deck: Create a short, high-level presentation (3-5 slides) that outlines our vision of the "Who Bought" layer, our cross-retailer aggregate strategy, and the first retail mandates we are securing. This is strictly for "informational" sharing, not a sales pitch. Goal: Informational Deck Ready

Week 3: Waiting for Retailer Momentum Sep 7 - Sep 13
  • Strategy

    Prepare Outreach Strategy: Draft the email templates for the initial outreach. The angle: "We are building the missing visual layer for POS measurement in CEE. We are locking in early retailer mandates and wanted to put ourselves on your radar." Goal: Outreach Templates Drafted

Week 4: Leverage SuperZoo Signing Sep 14 - Sep 20
  • Partner Outreach

    Incorporate SuperZoo into Pitch: Once SuperZoo is signed (per Retailer Plan), update the informational deck to include this specific traction. The conversation with Data Brokers is much stronger with a live, named retailer. Goal: Deck Updated with Traction

Week 5: OOO Ondrej Sep 21 - Sep 28
  • Out of Office

    Ondrej OOO: Hold off on initiating outreach to these high-value strategic targets until founder is available for calls.

Week 6: Post-OOO Outreach Initiation Sep 29 - Oct 4
  • Partner Outreach

    Launch Strategic Outreach: Send initial emails/LinkedIn messages to the mapped contacts at NielsenIQ, Circana, etc. Request a 15-minute introductory chat to share what we are building. Goal: Outreach Sent to Top 5 Targets

Week 7: Chasing Discovery Calls Oct 5 - Oct 11
  • Partner Outreach

    Follow-Up and Schedule: Follow up with non-responders. Secure at least one informational discovery call with an Innovation/M&A lead at a major data broker. Goal: 1 Informational Call Booked

Week 8: Executing the Informational Pitch Oct 12 - Oct 18
  • Partner Outreach

    Conduct Discovery Call: Execute the call. Present the vision of the cross-retailer aggregate and probe to see if they are exploring similar visual capabilities or facing client demand for "who bought" data. Goal: Gather Strategic Intelligence

Week 9: Leveraging Broker Dialogue for VCs Oct 19 - Oct 25
  • Research

    Summarize Broker Interest: Write a brief summary of the conversation with the data broker, noting their interest in the space and validating our strategic position as a future acquisition target. Goal: Summary Added to Data Room

Week 10: Momentum Update (EuroOil) Oct 26 - Nov 1
  • Partner Outreach

    Update Brokers on EuroOil: Once the EuroOil mandate is signed (per Retailer Plan) and the aggregate v1 is live, send a short "staying in touch" update to the broker contacts highlighting this expansion of our cross-retailer footprint. Goal: Keep Radar Active

Weeks 11-13: Supporting Fundraise DD Nov 2 - Nov 21
  • Partner Outreach

    Fundraising Support: Use the established contacts and summaries of discussions with data brokers to confidently answer VC due diligence questions regarding our long-term exit path and the viability of the 8-12x multiple. Goal: Validate Exit Path in DD
