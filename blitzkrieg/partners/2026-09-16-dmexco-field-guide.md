## 02 / Why go, and what this trip is not

**What it is not.** Our market is Czech Republic and Slovakia. German stores, German shoppers and
German brand budgets are not something we can serve before next year. Nobody at DMEXCO should hear
a price, a start date for German data, or a promise.

**What it is. Four jobs, in order of value:**

1. **Partners who need in-store proof.** In-store screen networks and retail media sellers who are
   being asked by advertisers for proof of effect at the shelf and at the till. We are the
   measurement layer under their screens, not a competitor to them.
2. **Routes into Czech and Slovak estates of German retailers.** REWE (Billa, Penny), Rossmann and
   OBI all run stores in CZ and/or SK and all send their retail media teams to Cologne. The goal is
   not a German deal. It is understanding who decides for the CEE subsidiaries, and a name there.
3. **Budget cartography at brands.** Several consumer brands with CEE budgets controlled from
   Germany or a regional hub are on stage. One question, asked well, tells us where a Czech shopper
   study would actually be signed.
4. **Market intelligence.** How the Germans are trying to standardize in-store measurement, and
   who is already linking in-store exposure to receipts.

**A good trip looks like this:**

- 3 or more partner conversations (Tier 1) with an agreed follow-up call and a named person.
- 2 or more "who decides for CZ/SK" answers from retailer groups (Tier 2).
- 5 or more budget-location answers from brands (Tier 3), logged verbatim.
- A one-page intelligence note on in-store measurement standards and similar approaches.
- Every contact logged within 24 hours (template in section 08).

## 05 / How to approach it

### This week, before Cologne (by Fri 18 Sep)

1. **Ticket and app.** Buy the ticket, install the app, complete the profile: *Partner, Minds &
   Models (Prague). In-store shopper measurement: who buys, receipt-verified, on existing store
   cameras.*
2. **Book the Tier 1 meetings now.** Speakers and booth staff fill their calendars before the show.
   Priority order: Retail Media Alliance / INOVISCO, Verve Retail Media, Zircon Tree, LAYA. Vistar
   only after checking with Josef.
3. **Send a short app or LinkedIn message** (template below) to the Tier 2 and Tier 3 names. Aim
   for a 15-minute coffee, not a booth demo.
4. **Tell Josef and Ondřej your list by Friday**, so nobody else from the company contacts the same
   people in parallel, and so they can flag anyone we already know.

**Message template (English, under 60 words):**

> Hi [name], I will be at DMEXCO and your session on [topic] caught my eye. I work with Minds &
> Models, a Prague company that measures who actually buys in physical stores, on cameras the store
> already has, with no facial recognition. I would value 15 minutes on how you prove in-store
> effect today. Coffee on Wednesday or Thursday?

### At the event

**The 30-second introduction:**

> "E-commerce runs on behavior. Physical retail got left behind, with receipts. We read who is
> shopping in a store, on the cameras the store already has, and match it to what they actually
> bought. No facial recognition, no identities, aggregated outputs only. We work in Czech Republic
> and Slovakia today."

**Then switch to the line for the person in front of you.** One stage per audience, never mixed:

| Talking to | Lead with | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| **In-store network, DOOH operator** | "You own the screens and the placement. We built the layer that is missing: who is in front of them, matched to what was bought." | How do you prove effect today? What do advertisers ask for that you cannot deliver? Would you test a measurement layer on your own estate? |
| **Retailer group** | "Your stores, your insight first, free. Brands only ever see anonymous, masked aggregates, and only with your approval." | Who decides this for your CZ and SK stores? Who should we talk to there? |
| **Brand** | "Syndicated data tells you what sold. We show you who buys it, in the aisle, receipt-verified." | The budget question (section 04, Tier 3). What do you buy today for in-store, and what can it not tell you? |
| **Agency** | "We make in-store media measurable for your clients, and we would rather work through you than around you." | Do your CEE offices buy in-store? Who runs it? |

**Germany-specific notes:**

- **"Camera" is a loaded word in Germany.** Say the privacy design in the same breath, every time:
  no facial recognition, no biometric templates, no identities, no tracking across visits,
  aggregates of 25 or more people only. Lead with what the store and the brand learn, then how.
- **Expect "can you do this in Germany?"** Honest answer: "Our focus today is Czech Republic and
  Slovakia. We would love to understand your market, and we can talk about Germany next year."
- **Expect "how is this different from LAYA / Cyreen?"** Answer: carts tell you that a cart passed
  a screen and what the receipt contained. We describe the shopper (who they are, in aggregate) and
  it works without special hardware in the store.
- **In-store networks will hear "personalization" or "screens" as competition.** Do not use either
  word about our offer. We measure their screens, we do not sell screens.

### After each conversation

- **Log it the same day** (section 08). Especially verbatim answers to the budget question.
- **No follow-up email with material** until Josef has seen the log. You open, we close.
- **Business card or badge scan plus one sentence** on what they need is enough for most
  Tier 4 and Tier 5 contacts.

## 06 / The rules (unchanged from the leave-behind, repeated because Cologne tempts)

| Rule | In Cologne this means |
|---|---|
| **Never quote a price** | "That depends on the coverage you would need, which is the first thing Josef will ask you." |
| **No exclusivity, ever** | Networks and retailers will ask for an exclusive partner. The answer is no, in any form, including "exclusive for Germany" |
| **No retailer's data before its mandate** | Do not name or imply any Czech or Slovak chain as a data source. Talk about the method, the privacy design and the categories we work in |
| **Schema yes, kitchen no** | Say what we deliver. Never how the models work, what hardware or which camera vendors, or accuracy internals |
| **Privacy line attached to every depth claim** | Always |
| **No internal status** | Nothing about funding, runway, pipeline, customer counts or deadlines. If asked how we are funded: brief, plain, back to the product |
| **You open, we close** | You book the follow-up and attend it. Josef or Ondřej runs the commercial part |

**The proof points you can use** (all already in your leave-behind): 450+ detectable visual
attributes, about 88 captured per shopper; 92% of orders at our pilot store matched to a shopper
profile. Do not name the pilot client.
