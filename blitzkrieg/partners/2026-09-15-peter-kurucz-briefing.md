## THE OBJECTIVE

**Debrief, not pitch.** He already said he's impressed — selling is wasted minutes.
One question governs the whole call:

> **"Would you have sold this, and would NIQ have bought it?"**

Everything else is downstream of his answer.

**Posture:** the who-layer goes *under* the what-layer. We are complementary to the incumbent —
a data input, a partnership, eventually an acquirer conversation. Push that future openly.
He is the first person who can tell us if it's real.

## THE DEBRIEF — three blocks, 14 minutes

### A. Would you have sold this? (his seat, his clients)

- In your last 12 months — **name a client request you couldn't fulfil.** What did they ask for, in their words?
- When a brand asked *"who actually buys my SKU standing at the shelf"* — **what did you sell them instead?**
- Shopper Loyalty: **what share of a chain's shoppers are actually carded?** What happens to the rest — do you report on them, model them, or drop them?
- Would you have put this in a pitch? Attached to what — **a module on an existing contract, or a new line?**
- **What would have made you not pitch it** — method, legal, price, or supply coverage?
- Of your 16 countries, **which buys this first, and why that one?**

### B. Could it be a data input to NIQ? (the partnership / acquirer path)

- **Does NIQ buy third-party data at all — and who owns those partnerships?** Corp-dev, insights leadership, or a data-sourcing function? *(This is BRK-4 — get the name.)*
- What **shape** must external data arrive in to be usable: licensed feed, derived read, fusion input, white-label module?
- **What's the commercial shape** — revenue share, per-country licence, minimum guarantee, or acquisition?
- **What kills a data partnership there** — legal/privacy review, the methodology board, or commercials?
- First meeting to signature, realistically — **how many months?**
- Has NIQ ever bought or partnered with an **in-store vision / visual analytics** player? What happened to it?
- **In-Store Vision** — what is it actually, who uses it, does it look at the shelf or the shopper?
- Does NIQ have **any answer for the non-carded shopper**? What do they say when a client asks?

### C. How did they work with data? (the internals that tell us where we plug in)

- The stack: POS feed, panel, loyalty — **where's the fusion layer, and what's weakest in it?**
- **Coverage/density threshold before NIQ will publish a number** — how many stores, how many observations? *(Calibrates our density escrow.)*
- Rough **price bands** for a shopper solution to a CEE brand. Bands, never contracts.
- Inside a brand, **who signs** for this — does trade marketing pay and insights veto, or is it different in his experience?
- **Renewal calendar** — when do these contracts actually get decided?
- Which **ex-NIQ people now sit inside brands** in CZ/SK? *(Fastest-converting buyer profile we have.)*

## HARD RULES

- **Stage 1 content only.** Method, schema, privacy architecture, self-benchmark kit, GymBeam parity,
  product shape, categories — yes. **Never:** retailer pipeline or counterparty names, internal
  benchmarks (**specifically the ~287 CZK/store/mo incumbent supply figure — INTERNAL-ONLY, we may
  never reveal we know it**), per-store pricing, taxonomy internals. *Schema yes, kitchen no.*
- **Label as you speak: SIGNED / IN NEGOTIATION / TARGET (C5).** He is the one listener who can
  falsify an overclaim, and he'd tell his old colleagues. Ambition is fine; ambition dressed as
  traction is not.
- **No superlatives.** Never "100× better than NIQ" — unfalsifiable, and it wakes the incumbent
  through the single channel most likely to carry it.
- **Never "personalization", "screens", "digital signage".** Puts us back in the AV category.
- **D1:** retailers pay in rights, never cash. If he's picturing commission on retailer sales,
  correct it in the room.
- **D7:** CZ + SK. His 16 EEMEA countries are a 2027 asset — say so, so he doesn't spend goodwill
  in the wrong place.
- **Nothing agreed on a first call.** No exclusivity, no territory, no title, no cash
  (€400/day rule). NDA before anything Stage 2 — not before this call.
- If he **volunteers NIQ confidential pricing or client lists unprompted** — note it. Useful, and
  it's exactly how he'd treat ours.
