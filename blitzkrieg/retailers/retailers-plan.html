The Retailer Win-Win Pitch

How to frame the deal to Retailers to avoid the "data theft" objection:

  • The Reframe: "We don't want to buy your data. We want to build you your own Retail Media Network (dunnhumby-style)."
  • The Firewall: "Your trading data (margins, sell-through) never leaves the building. We only package anonymized, aggregated audience data for brands."
  • The Commercials: "You monetize your audience and keep a portion of the revenue, but we take the infrastructure margin (≥ 60%) and retain unrestricted rights to the aggregate data."
  • The Benefit: "The same intelligence that earns you media revenue makes you a smarter buyer against those brands."
Target Retailer Profiles

Our primary pipeline targets to hit the 2 mandate goal:

  • SuperZoo (Pet Category): Best immediate target. Bundle the DPA with the 10-15 store expansion. Establishes "Seat Depth" in the pet sector.
  • EuroOil (Gas Station Category): 50 stores. Use brand pull (LOIs from JTI/PMI) to bypass the long sales cycle. Establishes "Stat-Sig" in the gas sector.
  • GymBeam / Teta: Secondary mid-tier targets to act as backups or further expansion.
  • Giants (Billa, COOP, Albert): 2-year CEO-level sales cycles. Start the conversation now, but do not rely on them for this round.
