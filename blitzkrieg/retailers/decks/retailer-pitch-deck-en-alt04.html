02 · How it works

Two data sources you already own. We join them and monetize them.

Visitor data and POS data sit apart today and neither of them earns anything. Only once they are joined does insight appear that brands will pay for.

Data sources
Audience data

Who is in the store: age, gender, group size, style, group dynamics. 88+ attributes per shopper, every visit. They are captured by unique Minds & Models technology, fully anonymously.

POS data

What actually sold: receipt, category, time of purchase. Your existing POS data, exactly as you hold it today.
