How it works

02 · How it works

Two data sources you already own. We join them and monetize them.

Visitor data and till data sit apart today and neither of them earns anything. Only once they are joined does insight appear that brands will pay for.

Data sources
Audience data

Who is in the store: age, gender, group size, style, group dynamics. 88+ attributes per shopper, every visit. They are captured by unique Minds & Models technology, fully anonymously.

POS data

What actually sold: receipt, category, time of purchase. Your existing till data, exactly as you hold it today.

Existing assets. Today they earn you nothing.
Processing
Unique AI technology

Our own AI models, built for exactly this, join the visit to the purchase. For the first time you know not only what sold, but also to whom. No one else on the market offers this layer today.

Output
AI chat

Ask in plain language and get the answer at once. No analyst, no waiting for an export.

Reports and dashboard

Demographics, footfall and conversion across your stores, refreshed daily.

This output is paid for by brands. For you it is free, forever.
