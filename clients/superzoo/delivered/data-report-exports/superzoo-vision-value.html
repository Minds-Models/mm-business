SuperZoo · POS × Visual Features · 8-week pilot

What the camera adds that the till cannot

POS records what sold. The visual layer identifies who bought it and how they shopped. Signals below exist only with the camera.

POS alone vs POS + visual features

POS alone

  • What sold, when, at which till
  • Basket size, value, mix
  • Promotions redeemed
  • Buyers only

+ Visual features

  • Shopper segment mix — no loyalty card
  • Behaviour — dwell, handling, engagement
  • Demographic signal by aisle
  • Non-buyers / footfall

Scope

  • 8-week SuperZoo pilot. 9,022 receipts; 7,815 linked to a checkout visit; 5,616 with visual features.
  • Linkage by departure-aligned timing at the checkout camera.
  • Per-attribute demographic lifts rest on small cells — directional, not forecasts.
  • Persona = visual segment inferred at the till; not an identity, no personal data.
