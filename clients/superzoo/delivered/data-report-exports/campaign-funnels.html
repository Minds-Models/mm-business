Why the effect can't be measured: the funnels

Each targeted segment, 2-week windows (pre 13–27 Jul → post 27 Jul–9 Aug). Every funnel starts at all store receipts and cascades down to how many receipts, from that segment's persona, actually bought the promoted product. Bars scaled to a shared axis; numbers are pre → post (green=up, red=down).

Ontario cat food → Men 40+ bought: 11 → 13
Leashes → Active Young bought: 2 → 4
Dog-treat 3+1 → Women 20+ bought: 30 → 16
Bulk value → Budget Conscious bought: 13 → 15
Ontario dog food → Retirees 60+ bought: 6 → 2
Cat-treat 3+1 → Students bought: 2 → 0
Two cuts destroy the power. (1) Narrowing from persona-tagged (~1,600) to a single targeted persona keeps only ~5–20% of receipts. (2) The promoted product's base rate is ~3–7% of baskets. Multiply them and you land at single-digit "bought" counts — where a ±2 change is pure noise. The campaign effect isn't absent so much as below this instrument's resolution at one store × two weeks.
