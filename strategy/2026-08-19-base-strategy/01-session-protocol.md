# Session protocol — how to work with this pack

**Applies to:** every future session (human or agent) doing business, strategy, sales, legal or
fundraise work for Minds & Models.

## 1. Before you produce anything

1. Read `00-INDEX.md` + this file. Load the one workstream file your task belongs to. Do not
   load the whole pack — context is cost.
2. Check `02-decisions.md` before proposing anything strategic. If your proposal contradicts a
   decision there, say so explicitly and argue it; never silently reverse a decision.
3. If your task touches pricing, breakeven, or the raise, read `03-business-model-fork.md` first.
   That question is open and dated — treat it as open.

## 2. Epistemic tags are binding

Every number you write carries one:

- `[FACT]` — founder fact base or a primary document on file (contract, RFI, counsel letter).
- `[EST]` — derived; **show the arithmetic or name the source** in the same sentence.
- `[ASSUME]` — assumption; **must carry a named kill-test** (what would disprove it, by when).
- `[DECIDED]` — a founder decision; cite the decision ID from `02-decisions.md`.

An untagged number in a strategy document is a defect. Never promote `[ASSUME]` to `[FACT]`
because it has been repeated; promotion requires the kill-test to have passed.

## 3. Every proposal ships with its kill-test

Owner · cost · time-to-signal · **the cheapest experiment that would disprove it**. A bold idea
without a kill-test does not go in a plan. We kill ideas fast; we do not avoid wild ones.

## 4. Hard guardrails (violating any of these fails the work)

- **Rights sequencing.** Never pre-sell or even hint at a retailer's data to a brand before a
  signed mandate for that retailer. Pre-mandate brand conversations are **methodology only**.
- **Trading-data firewall.** Retailer margins, sell-through and elasticity never reach a brand.
- **Benchmark integrity.** One chain in a category = a *channel read*, never a "market average".
  Cross-chain claims require ≥2 chains in that category.
- **Modeled vs real POS.** GymBeam and SuperZoo matching is real and citable. EuroOil POS in
  tobacco materials is **modeled** and must be labeled as such, every time.
- **Privacy language.** "Age estimation with error bands", never "age detection". Pair any depth
  claim with: no facial recognition, no identities, no biometric templates, aggregates only (k≥25).
  Do **not** claim the whole pipeline is anonymous — claim anonymous *outputs* from momentary
  processing. See `07-ws-legal-rights.md`.
- **Per-brand-worn claims are banned** company-wide until matched volume grows ~10× (fill is
  4–10%). Pooled claims only.
- **Internal-only numbers** (NIQ supply/demand benchmarks, our unit costs, competitor contract
  values) never appear in external material.
- **Forwardability litmus.** Every artifact must be safe if forwarded tonight to the client's
  retailer, a competitor, or a journalist.

## 5. Capacity is the scarce resource — respect it

Two founders sell ~1,000 hours total before 18 Feb. Dima is 8–10 h/week and **fully allocated**
(calibration first, P&G read second, nothing else before Nov). Any plan that implies more is
wrong on arithmetic, not on ambition. Standing rules: **max 8 live enterprise negotiations**;
**no new enterprise logo opened after 15 Nov**; parked for 90 days — O2, Vilgain, DOOH partners,
new pilot logos.

## 6. Spending discipline

At €12k/mo, **every €400 of speculative spend costs one day of company life.** Pre-raise
experiments are capped at ~€8k in total and each must de-risk the round itself. Everything
expensive sits behind the round closing.

## 7. After any external event (same day)

1. Full note to the CRM record; move the deal stage; set a next action with a date.
2. Commitments (ours and theirs) into the ledger with dates.
3. Update the relevant workstream file **only if the play changed** — not for every event.
4. If a gate in `10-calendar-gates.md` passed or failed, record the verdict there. A gate that
   silently slips is the failure mode this pack exists to prevent.
5. Follow-up draft in the counterparty's language (Czech for CZ retailers/brands, English for
   internationals and investors), linted against §4, approved before sending.

## 8. When you think the plan is wrong

Say so, with arithmetic. This pack was itself produced by re-deriving from first principles and
it found a €1.0M addition error in its own predecessor's revenue table. The correct response to
a suspect number is to recompute it and report the delta — not to inherit it.
