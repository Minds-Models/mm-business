## Cold-start context

- Brands-only architecture (D1, currently re-opened by the fork). Retailers receive the platform,
  reports and compliance pack free; they grant the rights rider. **Anchor at a price, discount to
  zero for rights** — never bid a naked €0 (see `03-business-model-fork.md`).
- One chain signature ≈ 200+ locations. The firehose is legal-commercial, not mechanical.
- Live procurement: SuperZoo / Plaček RFI — 206 CZ stores, existing HIKVISION estate + iVMS /
  HikCentral, 250+ CZ/SK licensing horizon, ~7bn CZK group turnover in pet. They asked for safety,
  operational efficiency and compliance — **no audience analytics was requested**; that is our
  attach, not their ask. Their own document carries a deep GDPR/AI-Act section — lead with the
  compliance pack, do not wait to be asked.
- Their incumbent integrator runs part of the warehouse estate. Treat as **delivery ally for
  phase-2 warehouse scope, not a rival bidder** (D10).
- Moris is a signed exclusive partner for their existing clients' fit-out projects: they pay our
  hardware install costs, they invoice the client, we invoice them off a pre-approved price list.
  Two live mechanisms: a **6-month clock** that frees us to approach a Moris client directly if
  they fail to advance a named opportunity after a written request, and a clause requiring
  client communications to route through them unless waived in writing (O1).
  Data contracts must always be **direct with the retailer**.
- Tobacco majors are not Moris clients — direct outreach breaches nothing.

## Standing rules

- **Max 8 live enterprise negotiations. No new enterprise logo after 15 Nov** (a 3-month
  procurement floor cannot close in-window). Parked 90 days: O2, Vilgain, DOOH partners, new logos.
- **Never approach a brand about a retailer's data before that retailer's mandate is signed.**
  Pre-mandate brand conversations are methodology-only.
- Sell **cohorts, not sequences**: one category roundtable, three founding seats offered on the
  spot with the density escrow visible. This is the main answer to the capacity ceiling.
- Every brand meeting opens with an artifact (parity deck, free mini-read, index sample), not a pitch.
- Discovery questions to get on record every time: *what do you buy today for this channel and
  what can't it tell you? at what coverage would this augment or replace your incumbent line?
  which budget and which owner buys it?*
- Cooperative and franchise groups multiply the sales cycle by the number of independent owners —
  a federation's headline store count is not a signature. Target company-operated chains with
  centralised IT.
