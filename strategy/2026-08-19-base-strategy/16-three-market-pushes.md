# Three markets × 35 pushes — the over-the-edge checklists

**How to use:** this is a MENU ordered by leverage, not a backlog. The capacity law stands
(one bottleneck, 8 accounts, ~1,000 founder-hours). Each week, the Monday protocol names the
bottleneck market; pick the highest un-done item from that market's list. Items marked ⚡ are
system-hacks (they remove a decision instead of winning it). Guardrails from
`01-session-protocol.md` apply to every item — especially: no pre-mandate brand data talk
(methodology only), internal benchmark figures never externally, no exclusivity ever.

## MARKET 1 — RETAILERS (they pay in rights)
*Edge to push them over: a signature. Physics: make yes cost nothing, make no feel like loss,
remove every step they'd have to do themselves.*

1. ⚡ Mandate signed IN THE ROOM — one page, Czech, our pen, their veto everywhere. Below any procurement threshold by design.
2. Pre-fill the mandate from facts.yaml before every meeting — zero fields for them to think about.
3. Say the firewall FIRST, unprompted: "your margins and sell-through never leave the building."
4. ⚡ RFI bid at €0 licence with the rights rider as the visible price tag — procurement can't compare it.
5. Week-1 PoC deliverable: fix their broken entry counters (their RFI admits the pixel-counting is wrong). Instant, checkable credibility.
6. Hand their DPO the finished compliance pack BEFORE being asked — converts the blocker into the internal champion.
7. Done-for-you DPIA + signage per chain — they need it for their own AI plans anyway; we arrive with homework complete.
8. The asymmetry demo: one page showing what their category manager knows before Purina does. Inverted leverage is the payment.
9. Offer the private-label carve-out proactively — the concession most likely to unlock SuperZoo, costs us nothing.
10. Ask their own-brand team "would YOU buy this?" — turns the data-fear holder into a buyer.
11. 90-day insight priority clause — they see everything before any brand does.
12. Publication lag (quarterly + delay) — strategic value intact, negotiation ammunition dead.
13. Prohibit the toxic cut in writing: named retailer × named brand vs competitor. Name it before they do.
14. Chain-masking by default; attribution only by per-cut written opt-in — and admit masking is thin at 2 chains.
15. Retrofit blitz: pooled-aggregate amendment to all 6 live pilots, consideration = one free insight report each.
16. ⚡ Moris §II.7 letter — starts the 6-month clock; slow partner becomes a countdown.
17. Moris §II.12 waiver request for direct data contracts — one email, removes a whole routing constraint.
18. ⚡ M2C flipped to delivery ally (warehouse scope) — the incumbent bidder becomes a reference instead of a rival.
19. Ask for read-only RTSP to 2–3 stores as "technical validation" — a yes that isn't a contract, but creates motion and S2 proof.
20. Gold-station trade: full station dashboard free forever for ONE receipt feed. Worth more than 49 installs.
21. ⚡ Priced pay-for-rights offer tabled at ČEPRO but not exercised — "on close of our round, X CZK/station/mo" — appetite logged, no cash out.
22. Data Act waiver clause standard in every site-host deal — the statutory rights position, never implicit.
23. Channel-leadership FOMO (market-level only, never name other pipelines): "the pet read will exist; the founding retailer sets its terms."
24. ⚡ Founding-retailer terms that expire — revenue share (max 40%) economics for the first N chains only. Scarcity on the supply side too.
25. Category-scoped mandate as the smaller ask when full-data stalls (pet only, tobacco only).
26. Monthly value memo to every live retailer: 3 things to change + what last month's advice earned. Revoking must feel like loss.
27. GymBeam case deck as show-don't-tell — real match rates, p-values, honest nulls.
28. "The market already does this to you, worse" — competitors' sales data is syndicated today, no veto, no masking. (Market fact only; no internal figures.)
29. Second retailer deliberately in a DIFFERENT channel — no rivalry, and masking strengthens for both.
30. Champion enablement kit: a board-ready slide the exec presents as THEIR new revenue stream. They sell it internally; we never pitch the board.
31. CPI portfolio rider — the landlord side-door that normalises being measured before any retailer rivalry exists.
32. Vending + own venues keep the corpus growing with zero retailer permission — a visible BATNA removes the desperation smell from every negotiation.
33. COOP: one call to COOP Centrum for the map (they can't sign, they can point); pick the one co-op with the youngest board.
34. Teta counter-play: "own your shopper layer instead of watching a competitor's data get monetised" — the sharpest pitch we hold.
35. Every retailer no gets the fallback ladder in order: retailer-only mode → category-limited rights → paid rights. Never a dropped aggregate clause.

## MARKET 2 — BRANDS (they pay in cash)
*Edge: the first paid seats. Physics: attach to a budget that already exists, sell scarcity that is
real, sell to cohorts not queues, let artifacts open every door.*

1. Tobacco on three channels AT ONCE: Moris letter + PM Slovakia direct + two cold letters to JTI/BAT trade marketing.
2. The anonymised sportstore report as the cold-outreach artifact — proof before pitch.
3. Start methodology-only conversations NOW — legal before any mandate, and it warms the cohort.
4. ⚡ P&G bucket hack: one quarterly mini-read FREE, in incumbent-shaped Excel, invoiced at €0 — the ask becomes "add a line at renewal," never "create a budget."
5. Founding seats: 3 per category, 50% off year 1, **floor €40k / list T1 from €75k** — scarcity that is written down, not implied.
6. ⚡ Density escrow printed in bold: fees refunded if store density misses by Q2-27. The risk objection becomes the trust proof.
7. ⚡ Category cohort day: one roundtable, 4–6 brands, three seats offered on the spot. One afternoon replaces six sales cycles.
8. ⚡ Two-document budget surfing: a 2026 study invoice (this year's money) + a 2027 subscription order (locked in Oct–Nov planning). One decision, two budget years.
9. LOI-for-early-access swap: "subject to retailer agreement" LOIs earn a 3-month custom-cut window. Never exclusivity.
10. Discovery questions on record, every meeting: what do you buy today for this channel? at what coverage does this replace it? who owns that budget?
11. Ask each brand its density requirement — their answer prices our ladder and makes them co-author of the product.
12. Gold station lands → tobacco read becomes receipt-calibrated → T2 pricing becomes honest. Chase it as a sales task, not a data task.
13. Every brand meeting OPENS with an artifact (parity deck, mini-read, index sample) — artifact first, pitch second.
14. Brand-Worn Index as the low-friction first purchase for apparel/sportswear — a research line item, no DPIA cycle, 6–10 week close.
15. Store Twin seat attached inside every seat negotiation — the add-on, never a separate sale.
16. Campaign measurement (P3) sold from MEDIA budget, not research budget — a second wallet in the same company.
17. Tobacco-funded screens as the trojan: €120/device split 3–4 ways is rounding error; the wave builds the estate their read runs on.
18. Normalise non-exclusivity with the currency frame: "works like syndicated measurement — every brand in the category buys the same read."
19. Vafo/Brit first study — the hungriest mid-tier; personas genuinely valuable there (Segment B).
20. Purina via the parent's habit: "your organisation already buys channel reads; this is the missing who-layer." (Never the internal figure.)
21. ⚡ Moris referral fee, 10–15% of year-1 on introduced seats — their tobacco relationships become commissioned salesforce.
22. One ex-NIQ/Kantar CZ person as fractional advisor — they know which budget owner and what the renewal calendar looks like.
23. Budget-owner map per brand BEFORE the meeting: insights vs trade marketing vs media. Ask for the right person by name.
24. Refuse exclusivity loudly and early — it signals currency ambition to brands and safety to retailers in the same sentence.
25. Honest nulls in every deliverable — compliance-heavy buyers reward it; one inflated number ends the relationship class.
26. "Age estimation with error bands", always — tobacco meetings live or die on compliance language.
27. PM Slovakia pilot seat even at T1 — the cross-border logo that makes the channel read "international" in every later deck.
28. Asahi/Prazdroj as the FMCG seed at co-op + fuel shops — beer/NAB is the convenience category with the most local pride.
29. ⚡ Free quarterly teaser index (top-line channel stats) to ~20 brand contacts — give the headline away, sell the depth.
30. One conference talk built on the parity artifact — inbound brand leads at near-zero founder cost.
31. Contracts DRAFTED before the meetings — legal review starts pre-pitch so signatures can land inside the Oct–Nov window.
32. Brand advisory micro-board: 2–3 insight managers shape the read's design for a free year-1 seat — people buy what they co-design.
33. First buyer gets named co-development credit (their choice) — cohort 2's social proof.
34. Track seats-per-slot weekly (target ≥3) — one buyer is a study, three is a currency; the metric forces the syndication behaviour.
35. Fallback if seats stall: reprice to 150–500k studies, convert to subscription at delivery. Land-expand, never discount the seat itself.

3. ⚡ The milestone contract to every warm fund: "by 15 Oct we deliver X, Y, Z, W — what specifically would you do?" Their gate, in writing.
4. ⚡ Evidence-drop cadence: one dated object every ~3 weeks (mandate → parity → RFI outcome → first seat). Never re-pitch; only send news.
5. Two-cohort narrative test — data-currency story vs floor+upside story, disjoint investor sets, measure second-meeting conversion.
6. ONE external number set (12-numbers-external.md) — the 9× contradiction is dead; keep it dead.
7. Deck v2 on the re-rating spine, velocity slide included ("here are the dated gates — check us").
8. Lock the open-link exit artifact TODAY; republish only from reconciled numbers.

10. Šimon Šepek worked as a connector — he produced Marek; ask him who else he'd put in front of us.
