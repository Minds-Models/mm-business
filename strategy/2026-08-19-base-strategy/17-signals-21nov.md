## 0 · THE HELICOPTER VIEW — the rule of one

The whole scoreboard generalizes to one sentence: **by 21 Nov, every currency must have converted
once.** One retailer paid in rights · one brand paid in cash · one investor paid in money · one
receipt-verified proof linking them · one inbound we didn't initiate. **One of each = the loop
turned = the strategy works.** Zero of any one = that market's premise is untested or dead.

**FLOOR vs TRAJECTORY — do not confuse them.** "One of each" is the floor: it proves the loop
turns. It is NOT on-track for February. The calendar physics: after 21 Nov only ~11 selling weeks
remain (Czech B2B dies 18 Dec–2 Jan), and the Oct–Nov brand-budget window is already CLOSED — a
brand not captured by then buys from locked 2027 budgets. So the trajectory metric at 21 Nov is
not "brands paid"; it is **2027 subscription orders banked inside the budget window** (the
two-document founding seat exists precisely for this):

| Feb outcome | needs at 21 Nov |
|---|---|
| Floor (~€0.5M) | 1 paying brand, thin order book |
| **Base case €0.8–1.4M** | **2–3 paying + ≥4 banked 2027 orders/LOIs-with-numbers** |
| Operating target €4.40M | 5–6 brand contracts signed + tobacco block in contracting + index sold |

One paying brand with an empty 2027 order book on 21 Nov = strategy validated, plan landing at
floor. The order book, not the invoice count, is November's forward-looking brand signal.

The goal ladder it hangs from:
**5 yr** — be the who-bought measurement currency (re-rate to a €100–150M outcome) →
**18 mo** — own what earns the multiple: cross-retailer rights + recurring brand ARR →
**6 mo (18 Feb)** — one full turn at scale: the asset scoreboard + first slots live →
**3 mo (21 Nov)** — **one of each.**

**How to read this:** ARR is lagging at this horizon — the loop turns at most once by 21 Nov.
Judge the *turn*, not the revenue. 🟢 = the node turned · 🟡 = turned partially or a kill-test
fired **with a verdict and a reallocation** (that is the system working) · 🔴 = a gate passed its
date **without a verdict**, or the premise itself was disproven. Review dates: 5 Sep · 30 Sep ·
15 Oct (the fork) · 15 Nov · **21 Nov (this scoreboard, full pass)**.
