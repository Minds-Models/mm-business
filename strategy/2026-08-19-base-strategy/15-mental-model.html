Minds & Models · mental model · internal · 20 Aug 2026

One loop. Three markets. Three clocks.

The strategy switch in one thought: we stopped selling a product down a pipeline and started spinning a loop. Rights unlock data, data becomes proof, proof sells brand seats, paid brands pull the next retailer in — and every turn throws off evidence that retailers, brands and investors all trade against. Capital doesn't build the company; it buys turns of the loop before the clocks run out.

Law 1 · one bottleneck

At any moment exactly one node is the constraint — pushing any other node is wasted founder-hours. Today it is RIGHTS: nothing downstream monetises until a signature exists.

Law 2 · turns compound

Each turn is cheaper than the last: proof is reusable across every chain, masking strengthens with each retailer, and PULL replaces outbound with inbound. The first turn is the expensive one — we are in it now.

Law 3 · evidence clears three markets

The same artifact advances a retailer deal, a brand deal and a fund conversation at once. Work that produces no evidence object this month is probably the wrong work.

The Monday protocol — three questions, every week

Which node is the bottleneck this week?

Name exactly one. All selling hours go there. If two founders disagree on which, that is the meeting.

What evidence object does this week's work produce, and who receives it?

Retailer, brand, or fund — ideally all three. No object in sight ≈ wrong work.

Which clock is nearest, and does it change the answer to Q1?

The clocks overrule elegance. A weaker move before the budget window beats a perfect one after it.
