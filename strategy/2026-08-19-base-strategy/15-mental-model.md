# The mental model — one loop, three markets, three clocks

**Purpose:** the compression of the whole strategy into something two founders (or a fresh
session) can hold in working memory and use to make Monday decisions. Visual version published as
the "One Loop, Three Markets" artifact; this file is the text twin.

## The one thought

**We stopped selling a product down a pipeline and started spinning a loop.**

```
        ┌────────── 5 · PULL ──────────┐
        │  paid brands make retailer   │
        ▼  №+1 say yes (RFI = proof)   │
1 · RIGHTS → 2 · DATA → 3 · PROOF → 4 · REVENUE
mandate→licence  their cameras   POS×visual     slots × seats
(0 signed —      our software    parity + law   10 × €500k ≈ €5M
 THE bottleneck)  ~€0/store      (the moat)

   CAPITAL (bridge → A/B fork) = starter motor, buys turns — not a node
```

Three markets trade against the loop, each in its own currency:
**retailers pay in RIGHTS** (never cash — D1) · **brands pay in CASH** (annual seats) ·
**investors pay in TIME + MONEY**. All three accept the same tender: **evidence objects** —
mandate (≤5 Sep) · parity (19 Sep / 10 Oct) · RFI outcome (Oct) · first seat (≤6 wks post-mandate).

## The three laws

1. **One bottleneck.** At any moment exactly one node constrains the loop; founder-hours anywhere
   else are wasted. Today: RIGHTS. Expected migration: RIGHTS (Sep) → REVENUE/seats (Oct–Nov) →
   DATA/deployment (Dec–Feb) — but *observe*, don't assume.
2. **Turns compound.** Proof is reusable across chains, masking strengthens with each retailer,
   PULL converts outbound into inbound. The first turn is the expensive one; we are in it now.
   This is also the honest answer to "why won't the next 8 months look like the last 8" — the old
   model had no loop: every sale started from zero.
3. **Evidence clears three markets at once.** Work that produces no dated evidence object this
   month is probably the wrong work. One object every ~3 weeks is what "fast-paced" looks like
   from the outside.

## The Monday protocol (use verbatim in the all-hands)

1. **Which node is the bottleneck this week?** Name exactly one. All selling hours go there.
   If the founders disagree on which, *that disagreement is the meeting.*
2. **What evidence object does this week's work produce, and who receives it?** Retailer, brand,
   or fund — ideally all three. Nothing in sight ≈ wrong work.
3. **Which clock is nearest, and does it change Q1's answer?** Clocks overrule elegance: end-Aug
   SuperZoo · 30 Sep tobacco/bridge · **15 Oct fork** · Oct–Nov budget lock · mid-Dec cash wall ·
   18 Feb tally.

## What this model deliberately leaves out

Team/ops cadence (`09`), decision history (`02`), research evidence (`11`). A mental model is a
steering instrument, not an inventory — when it stops matching reality (e.g. the bottleneck stops
migrating as predicted, or a market refuses its currency), update *this file first*, then the plan.
