
## Addendum, 17 Sep 2026, second sitting: a slower start, a faster 2027, and the MARKETS tab put in order

Three founder instructions after the profitability addendum.

**The start.** 172k of ARR in December 2026 (four founding seats sold within three months of the
first chain going live) read as an overpromise, and the founder wanted a slower first year with a
faster second. Measured: first paying seat four months after a category goes live (the pilot
quarter) takes December 2026 to the legacy 12k, with the first four founding seats landing in
January 2027, and costs only 0.14M of 2031 ARR; halving the founding seats instead would have cost
1.6M and needed a larger pre-seed. Penetration maturing in 18 months rather than 24 (the six payable
brands per category are known by name, so the ramp is a sales calendar, not a discovery process)
lifts 2027 to 808k and 2031 to 21.59M. Both were taken. Opening Slovakia and Poland two or three
months earlier, ahead of the seed, would lift 2027 to about 970k and is left as a founder call
(walkthrough, open point 12).

**The United Kingdom in order.** The UK row had been appended after the United States in the market
table, the monthly build and the T2 block, and its monthly rows sat outside the collapsed group. The
rows were moved into entry order (Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, DACH, France with Benelux, the United
Kingdom, the Nordics with Iberia, the United States last as the scenario-only row), the preset
columns re-ordered to match (G UK, H Nordics, I US), the scenario check and the phase text
re-pointed, and the three collapsed groups rebuilt so that every per-market row hides with its
neighbours. References followed the moves; the totals and the CHECKS lines were re-read afterwards.

**The category inventory block** at the foot of MARKETS is now a section with the same header bar as
the monthly build, collapsed by default (the plus sign opens the channel table, the weighted totals,
the model-input note, and the two prose rows, now labelled "How to read this" and "Worked example,
Czechia" in the label column).

**The chart** on SUMMARY is re-based to December 2027 = 100, because December 2026 now carries only
the legacy line; the caption and the chart title say so.
