01 · What you decide every year

Three decisions that shape your center,
made today on how many, not on who.

01
Tenant mix and leasing

Which tenants fit the people who actually walk in? What evidence do you bring to a renewal, a relocation or a new lease?

Today: door counts, periodic surveys, tenant sales.

02
Programming and marketing

Which events, campaigns and weekend activations bring the audiences you want, and at what time do those audiences arrive?

Today: traffic before and after, and experience.

03
Pricing space and media

What is an entrance, a promo zone or a screen worth, based on who passes it rather than only how many?

Today: footfall per entrance, sold as volume.

The shift

Footfall already underpins how your assets are valued and marketed. The next question is not how many people came. It is who they were.
