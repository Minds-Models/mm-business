The Data

03 · What We Actually Collect

This is the resolution brands pay for.

Every visit becomes structured, POS-matched attributes: de-identified and aggregated before they ever leave the store. Here's the level of detail, and who's already paying for it.

What we actually capture

  • Who's there: age band, gender, group size, solo or family
  • How they shop: path through the store, dwell time by aisle, repeat visits
  • What they do: pickup and put-back, basket size, conversion by aisle
  • Matched to POS: which visits became a sale, and of what
