Trade marketing · the promotional line
Which shoppers am I discounting who would have bought anyway?
Sell-out is your only proof that a promotion worked, and it arrives weeks later in the chain’s own format. It cannot tell the display from the leaflet from the week.
Why it is hard: in sell-out, the two look identical.
Category and KAM · the annual negotiation
Who walked out without my category, and what did they buy instead?
Scan data says what sold. A 2,000-household panel says what people remember buying. Neither one was standing next to the shopper who chose somebody else.
Why it is hard: that shopper is in no file you own.
Brand marketing · the brief
My brief targets under-30s. Who is actually at the shelf?
Your target definition came from a tracker, a panel and the agency’s segmentation, and every one of them describes a household rather than a person in an aisle.
Why it is hard: you are briefing on a stranger.
