The Gap

01 · The Gap You Live With

Syndicated data tells you what sold. It has never told you who buys.

Your evidence about the store is a receipt and a survey. The receipt has no person attached to it. The survey has a person, but not the one standing in the aisle at 17:40 on a Thursday. Between them sits the moment that actually decides your category, and it is unmeasured.

Sell-out arrives without a shopper

POS and panel feeds give you units, share and price. They cannot tell you which shopper picked you up, which one picked up the competitor, or which one walked past both without stopping.

Panels measure what people say

Claimed behaviour, recalled after the fact, from a sample projected onto a market. Useful for attitudes, weak for the two seconds in which a hand reaches for a pack.

In store is the unmeasured medium

Every other channel you buy reports who saw it and who converted. In-store activation is still judged against a baseline weeks later, with no idea who was exposed.

The Data

02 · What We Actually Measure

This is the resolution the read is built from: over 450 visual attributes.

What we capture

  • Who is there: age band estimated with error bands, gender, group size, solo or family
  • How they shop: route through the store, dwell by aisle, pickup and put-back, repeat traffic
  • What they present as: style, garment category, pooled brand-worn signals
  • Matched to POS: which visit became which receipt line, and which did not convert
Illustrative example
Pet aisle, Thursday 17:41: female, 25–34 band, 51s dwell, two SKUs handled, one purchased. Aggregated across every store and every day, published only at cell sizes of 25 or more.

How the read reaches you

  • The category read: segment by SKU by daypart by store cluster, on a fixed refresh cycle
  • Custom cuts on the questions your team is actually arguing about internally
  • A dashboard and a chat interface: ask in plain language, no analyst in the loop
  • A method note every time: what is measured, what is modeled, error bands on both
Built for your insights lead
Nothing in the read is a black box. Targets and holdouts are pre-registered before we run, and we report the nulls alongside the findings.
No facial recognition No biometric templates No cross-visit tracking Images never leave the store
