# Boilerplate — company blurbs & standard paragraphs

## Company blurb (EN)

**25 words:** Minds & Models teaches physical stores who their customers are — in-store
behavior captured visually, matched to purchases (92%), fully anonymous. The measurement
layer physical retail never had.

**50 words:** Minds & Models is building the measurement layer for physical retail. Our
in-store vision AI describes every shopper across 88+ attributes — style, brands worn,
life stage — and matches 92% of purchases to a shopper profile, with zero identification.
Stores finally learn who buys, not just what sold. GDPR-native by design.
